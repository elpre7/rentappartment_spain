# Nuevos grados y aumentos de plazas de nuevo ingreso en España (2023-24 a 2027-28): mapa por ciudades para inversión en vivienda estudiantil

Research date: 18 October 2026. Method note: direct page fetching was blocked in this environment (DNS/proxy 403), so the findings below come from search-engine summaries of the cited pages. They have not been checked against the full article text, so verify key figures (especially per-university tables) before relying on them. "CONFIRMED" means implemented, published in BOE/official notice, or students already enrolled. "ANNOUNCED" means a plan or approval with no enrolment verified yet.

## 1. State-funded increases in Medicina plazas (Ministerio de Sanidad programme, 2023-24 to 2026-27)

### Takeaway
Between 2023-24 and 2025-26 the Ministerio de Sanidad paid public universities to raise first-year Medicina intake by up to 15% over their verified capacity. That added about 677 first-year plazas in 2023-24 and about 922 in each of 2024-25 and 2025-26. For 2026-27 state money only maintains the plazas already created and funds no new first-year increases, so further growth now comes from regions, new degrees and private universities. The funding came through Sanidad Royal Decrees, not through a CGPU agreement specific to Medicina.

### Cited Findings
- **2023-24 (CONFIRMED):** Sanidad funded 677 additional Medicina plazas with €49,998,257.74, mostly for faculty infrastructure and equipment. Then-minister José Miñones described it as an increase of up to 15% in new entrants. The press note is dated 25 or 30 September 2023. — [Sanidad nota 6235](https://www.sanidad.gob.es/gabinete/notasPrensa.do?id=6235); [El Debate, 30/09/2023](https://www.eldebate.com/educacion/20230930/sanidad-financia-casi-50-millones-creacion-677-nuevas-plazas-grado-medicina-15-mas_143439.html)
- In July 2023 Sanidad had announced 706 new places. The final figure was 677. — [Redacción Médica, 30/09/2023](https://www.redaccionmedica.com/formacion/estudiantes/20230930/sanidad-oficializa-plazas-extra-de-medicina-para-este-curso/212063_0.html)
- El Debate put public-university Medicina supply at 6,626 plazas in 2023-24, about 15% more than the year before. — [El Debate](https://www.eldebate.com/educacion/20230930/sanidad-financia-casi-50-millones-creacion-677-nuevas-plazas-grado-medicina-15-mas_143439.html)
- Example allocations for 2023-24: ULPGC (Las Palmas) received +15 plazas and €714,825. Universidad de La Laguna added no plazas but received €120,000. — [RTVC](https://rtvc.es/sanidad-anuncia-15-nuevas-plazas-grado-medicina-ulpgc/)
- **2024-25 (CONFIRMED, BOE):** Real Decreto 874/2024 (3 September 2024) funds up to 1,548 plazas: 922 first-year plazas within each university's 15% cap, plus 626 second-year plazas carried over from 2023-24. The budget is €23.22M (€13.83M for first year, €9.39M for second year). — [BOE-A-2024-17784](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2024-17784); [Consalud](https://www.consalud.es/formacion/gobierno-financia-1000-nuevas-plazas-medicina-reparto-por-universidades_148074_102.html)
- Per Consalud, the largest 2024-25 allocations were Universidad de Santiago de Compostela, Universidad del País Vasco and Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona, at **+53 first-year plazas each**. I could not see the full Annex I table. — [Consalud](https://www.consalud.es/formacion/gobierno-financia-1000-nuevas-plazas-medicina-reparto-por-universidades_148074_102.html)
- Gaceta Médica lists Andalucía, Madrid and Galicia as the regions receiving the most funding. Its article mixes up the academic year. — [Gaceta Médica](https://gacetamedica.com/profesion/plazas-medicina-comunidad-autonoma/)
- Ministry offer for 2024-25: 6,664 public plus 1,988 private plazas, 8,652 in total, of which 77% are public. — [Consalud](https://www.consalud.es/formacion/aumentan-plazas-en-grado-medicina-en-espana-hasta-alcanzar-8652-77-publicas.html)
- **2025-26 (CONFIRMED, BOE):** Real Decreto 901/2025 (7 October 2025) gives €26.7M for 1,783 plazas: 922 new first-year plazas, 642 second-year plazas maintained, and 219 third-year plazas. The split is €13.83M, €9.63M and €3.28M. The Consejo de Ministros approved the spending limits in November 2025 (€26,745,000). — [BOE RD 901/2025](https://www.boe.es/eli/es/rd/2025/10/07/901); [Sanidad nota 6762](https://www.sanidad.gob.es/gabinete/notasPrensa.do?id=6762); [Hacienda](https://www.hacienda.gob.es/es-es/prensa/noticias/noticiaspdf//368.pdf)
- Example for 2025-26: Universidad de Zaragoza filled all 220 Medicina plazas it offered. — [Unizar](https://academico.unizar.es/sites/academico/files/archivos/acceso/convo/procextramed2025.pdf)
- Ministry total for 2025-26 per Consalud: 8,212 Medicina plazas. Example university figures: Almería 69, Cádiz 154. The 8,212 total does not match the 8,652 given for 2024-25, so the basis of the two figures may differ. — [Consalud 2025/26](https://www.consalud.es/formacion/estas-son-las-plazas-de-medicina-que-oferta-cada-universidad-en-el-curso-2025-2026.html)
- **2026-27:** The state programme now only funds maintenance of the extra plazas already created. It provides no money for new first-year increases. — [ULL news](https://www.ull.es/portal/noticias/2026/la-universidad-de-la-laguna-aumenta-hasta-150-las-plazas-de-acceso-a-medicina/)
- Universidad de La Laguna (Tenerife) is raising Medicina from 130 to 150 first-year plazas for 2026-27 only, funded from its own resources. It received almost 1,500 applications. — [ULL](https://www.ull.es/portal/noticias/2026/la-universidad-de-la-laguna-aumenta-hasta-150-las-plazas-de-acceso-a-medicina/)
- In June 2026 Sanidad said it plans to include more funding for Medicina and Enfermería plazas in the 2027 Presupuestos Generales del Estado (PGE). This is ANNOUNCED only. — [Redacción Médica, 11/06/2026](https://www.redaccionmedica.com/politica/ministerio-sanidad/20260611/sanidad-llevara-mas-plazas-de-medicina-enfermeria-los-ansiados-pge/312101_0.html)
- Students (CEEM) and deans opposed the latest expansion. CEEM notes that Medicina graduates rose from 6,513 in 2018 to 8,552 in 2024 while MIR places grew more slowly. — [Consalud](https://www.consalud.es/formacion/estudiantes-decanos-se-oponen-ultima-ampliacion-plazas-medicina-no-es-coherente_148553_102.html); [CEEM position paper, March 2026](https://ceem.org.es/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Posicionamiento-Nuevas-Facultades.pdf)

### Inferences
- The state programme raises first-year intake by about 922 per year from 2024-25. As those cohorts move up, the extra students in the six-year degree build to roughly 4,000-5,000 more Medicina students across public faculties by about 2028-29, assuming the plazas are kept. The effect is concentrated in existing large faculties: Santiago, Bilbao/Leioa, Barcelona/Bellaterra, and the Madrid and Andalusian universities.
- Because each university can add at most 15%, the increases follow existing faculty size. For a mid-size city, about +10-20 Medicina students per cohort has only a marginal effect on housing demand.

### Gaps
- I could not obtain the full per-university Annex I tables for RD 874/2024 or RD 901/2025 because fetching was blocked. Next step: read the BOE annexes.
- I found no CGPU agreement specific to Medicina plaza increases for 2026-27. The CGPU approves the general offer of plazas each year (LOSU art. 15.1.e), as cited in a Murcia order: [BORM 2026](https://www.borm.es/services/anuncio/ano/2026/numero/2179/pdf?id=842861).
- I found no verified national total of Medicina plazas for 2026-27.

## 2. New Medicina degrees and faculties: which are real, and when they start

### Takeaway
Confirmed new public Medicina degrees in the period are León (80 plazas) and Burgos (about 134-159 enrolled), both starting 2026-27, and La Rioja/Logroño (30 plazas, 2026-27). Jaén, Almería and Huelva are not new: their degrees started in 2022-2024 and are still adding cohorts until about 2027-28 to 2029-30. New private degrees are at San Jorge (Zaragoza, 2025-26), CEU Fernando III (Sevilla, 2025-26), Ramon Llull/Blanquerna and Abat Oliba CEU (Barcelona, 2026-27). Catalonia also plans a public expansion including a new UB-led project in Terrassa from 2027-28.

### Inferences
- **Strongest new-cohort signals for housing demand:** Burgos (about 134-159 Medicina freshmen a year, building over six years to roughly 800-950 students if sustained) and León (80 a year, building to about 480). Logroño adds 30 a year, about 180 at full build-out. Jaén, Almería and Huelva are still adding cohorts until full build-out around 2027-2030.
- In Barcelona, the new private degrees add 170 plazas a year from 2026-27 (about 1,000 students at full build-out). Terrassa is a mid-size satellite city with a new public Medicina cohort from 2027-28 (announced).
- High-scoring Medicina students often move away from home, because cut-offs above 12.6 send them wherever they are admitted. That makes new faculties in mid-size cities likely drivers of rental demand.

### Gaps
- I found nothing in this search round on Medicina at Vigo, Girona or Lleida (both already have Medicina), UPCT/Cartagena, or a new Rey Juan Carlos campus. I could not verify any new public Medicina degree in these places for 2023-2027, so treat them as UNVERIFIED.
- I could not confirm the 2027-28 public Catalan allocation by campus beyond Terrassa.

## 3. Enfermería, Fisioterapia, Veterinaria, Ingenierías and other new grados in mid-size cities

### Takeaway
The clearest new non-Medicina degree is Veterinaria at Universidad de Salamanca (90 plazas, started 2026-27), with a public Veterinaria degree also announced for Ontinyent (Universitat de València). ## 4. Cities with the largest growth in nuevo ingreso and matriculados; international and Erasmus inflows

### Takeaway
National totals are at record levels: about 1.9 million university students in 2025-26 (all levels), 21% more than ten years ago. Almost all of the growth is in private universities and másteres. I could not obtain a city-by-city growth table from SIIU or EDUCAbase, or per-university Erasmus inflow figures, in this round.

### Cited Findings
- 2025-26 enrolment is a record of almost 1.9 million (grado, máster and doctorado), +21% on a decade earlier. Almost 30% of students are at private universities, up from 15% ten years ago. — [Ministerio de Ciencia, "Datos clave", 07/10/2025](https://www.ciencia.gob.es/dam/jcr:b70dd7bb-cbd2-4efb-b4fe-e69059986611/DatosClave_SIU.pdf)
- The full "Datos y cifras del SUE 2025/26" report is the primary source for by-university and by-region detail. — [DatosCifras_SUE2025_2026.pdf](https://www.ciencia.gob.es/dam/jcr:747444d8-7089-4b15-97e1-0fc27874e6a7/DatosCifras_SUE2025_2026.pdf)
- In 2024-25 Medicina had ten applications per plaza, the highest demand ratio of any degree. — [Consalud](https://www.consalud.es/formacion/medicina-el-grado-universitario-mas-demandado-en-espana-por-cada-plaza-ofertada-hay-diez-solicitudes.html)
- Erasmus+ 2025 (Spain): about 154,000 participants across all sectors and €377M or more. Higher education had 1,024 projects and 27,819 mobilities, the most projects of any country. — [Ministerio de Ciencia, July 2025](https://www.ciencia.gob.es/Noticias/2025/julio/erasmus-2025-dotacion-historica-morant.html)
- Spain is among the leading receivers of incoming Erasmus+ students in Europe. — [Navarra Información, 27/05/2025](https://www.navarrainformacion.es/2025/05/27/espana-uno-de-los-lideres-en-recepcion-de-estudiantes-erasmus-en-europa/)
- An undated secondary source says Complutense is the second Spanish university by Erasmus traffic. Granada, Complutense and Valencia are traditionally the top Erasmus universities. Low reliability. — [Sinmaletas](https://sinmaletas.com/blog/8-universidades-de-espana-con-mas-estudiantes-erasmus/)

### Inferences
- From my background knowledge, which I could not verify this round, Granada, Valencia, Madrid, Sevilla and Barcelona are the usual leading Erasmus host cities. Treat this as a hypothesis to check against SEPIE data.
- Most of the growth in matriculados since 2022 is in private universities and másteres, which are concentrated in Madrid, Barcelona and Valencia. Most new public grados are in mid-size cities.
