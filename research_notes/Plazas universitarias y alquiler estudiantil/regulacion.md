# Regulatory and tax risk: alquiler por habitaciones / de temporada to students

## 1. Zonas de mercado residencial tensionado (Ley 12/2023): where they are declared and what rent caps apply

### Takeaway
At 30 Sep 2026 about 317 municipalities had declared stressed zones (zonas tensionadas). They are in only five autonomous communities: Cataluña with 271 (about 89% of the total), Navarra 21, País Vasco 18, Asturias 5 and Galicia 2 (A Coruña and Santiago). Madrid, Andalucía, the Comunitat Valenciana, Castilla y León and the other communities have declared none. In a stressed zone, any landlord's new contract is capped at the previous rent for that home. Large landlords (grandes tenedores), and homes not let in the last 5 years, are also capped by the State reference-price index.

## 2. Regulation of alquiler de temporada and alquiler por habitaciones

### Takeaway
**National level:** RDL 28/2026 and 29/2026 (in force from 8 Oct 2026, with some provisions deferred) reform the LAU, the national tenancy law:
- Temporada contracts need a real, documented cause; studies count. They must last more than 31 days and, as a rule, no more than 12 months.
- Without a real cause, chained contracts, or more than 12 months, the contract becomes a main-residence lease of 5 years (7 years if the landlord is a company).
- Room rentals are brought under the LAU. The sum of the rents for the rooms cannot exceed the rent for the whole flat (new LAU art. 17.9).

All of this is **provisional until the 12-Nov Diputación Permanente vote, and politically fragile**. Ley 11/2025 in Cataluña is in force from 1 Jan 2026 and already applies these caps there. It has been challenged before the Constitutional Court (TC) but not suspended. The national short-term rental registry (Registro Único, RD 1312/2024) was annulled by the Supreme Court in May 2026.

- **A temporada contract is valid only if it has a real, documentable cause** stated in writing: work, studies, medical treatment, etc. Its duration is from 32 days to one year. ### Inferences
- **Registry.** The annulled registry covered "corta duración" lets, meaning tourist and temporada lets advertised on platforms. While it was in force it did cover temporada lets advertised online, which is background knowledge and should be verified. After STS 620/2026 there is no enforceable national registration number. Regional tourism registries still apply to tourist lets, not to student temporada lets.
- **Student temporada lets.** A September–June student contract with a stated study cause and the enrolment documented looks like it still fits the new temporada definition (31 days to 12 months). The risk is in renewals: chaining contracts with the same student for a second year could convert the lease into a 5- or 7-year main-residence lease.
- **Rooms.** The new national whole-flat cap does not by itself limit rent unless a whole-flat cap exists, as in a stressed zone or Cataluña. In non-stressed regions (Madrid, Andalucía, Valencia) its practical bite is uncertain. Emprendedores notes the legal gap.
- **Scenario risk:**
  - (a) The RDLs are convalidated on 12 Nov and become law; they could be processed as a bill and amended.
  - (b) They fall, and the prior regime returns: free-form temporada contracts under LAU art. 3, and no national room cap.
  - (c) A new government after 29 Nov changes direction, either way.
- Cataluña's regime applies regardless of the national outcome unless the Constitutional Court annuls it.

## 3. Tax treatment: IRPF reduction, VAT, ITP

### Takeaway
- **DGT doctrine (Dirección General de Tributos, the tax ministry's binding rulings):** a room let qualifies for the IRPF main-residence rental reduction (V0412-25) **only** if each room is the tenant's permanent main residence. A September–June student contract is classed as temporada and gets **no reduction** (V3162-21, V0444-24).
- **RDL 29/2026** redesigns the reduction for new contracts (reportedly from 1 Dec 2026): the general rate is 50%; higher rates of 70–100% apply if the rent is cut by more than 5% or is below the index; lower rates of 15–40% apply if the rent rises. It also adds a 10% VAT rate for furnished lets of up to 30 nights. All of this is pending convalidation.
- **VAT:** student housing without hotel-type services is VAT-exempt.

### Cited Findings
- **DGT V0412-25 (20 Mar 2025):** renting by rooms does not prevent the 50% IRPF reduction for housing rentals, provided the rooms are the tenants' main residence, not temporada or daily lets. The residential use must be provable, not merely stated in the contract. — [idealista](https://www.idealista.com/news/fiscalidad/2025/07/23/854027-irpf-se-puede-aplicar-la-reduccion-por-vivienda-habitual-alquilando-habitaciones); [EMS](https://www.ems-ir.com/consulta-vinculante-v0412-25-hacienda-admite-reduccion-del-50-en-arrendamientos-por-habitaciones/); [Primera Lectura](https://primeralecturaediciones.com/consultas/archivo/el-arrendamiento-por-habitaciones-permite-aplicar-la-reduccion-del-50-en-irpf-si-se-acredita-el-destino-a-vivienda-habitual/)
- **DGT V3162-21 (21 Dec 2021):** a home let to students from September to June — "en ningún caso resultará aplicable la reducción" (the reduction will in no case apply). **V0444-24 (18 Mar 2024)** keeps this criterion; the tax agency treats such leases as temporada. — [SuperContable](https://www.supercontable.com/informacion/impuesto_renta_IRPF/Consulta_vinculante_de_la_DGT_V3162-21..html); [Iberley V3162-21](https://www.iberley.es/resoluciones/resolucion-vinculante-direccion-general-tributos-v3162-21-21-12-2021-11554272); [Jorge Borrajo](https://jorgeborrajo.com/alquiler-a-estudiantes-y-reduccion-en-el-irpf/)
- **RDL 29/2026, new IRPF scale.** All rates are reductions of net rental income.
  - *New contract with the initial rent cut by more than 5%, landlord not a large landlord:* 100%, 95%, 90%, 85% or 70%, depending on tenant continuity, rent level, stressed-zone location and tenant age.
  - *Same or lower rent:* 50%.
  - *Higher rent:* 40% (rise up to 5%), 30% (up to 10%), 25% (up to 15%), 20% (up to 20%), 15% (above 20%).
  - *First letting of a home:* 100%, 95%, 90%, 85% or 50%; the 100% rate requires a rent below the index.
  - *Social or public-programme lets:* 70%.
  - *Rehabilitated homes:* 60% (one source).
  - *Existing contracts:* contracts signed up to 1 Dec 2026 keep their prior reduction.
  — [economistjurist](https://www.economistjurist.es/articulos-juridicos-destacados/medidas-tributarias-del-real-decreto-ley-29-2026-sobre-vivienda/); [Iberley](https://www.iberley.es/noticias/nuevas-medidas-fiscales-vivienda-rd-ley-29-2026-37071); [fiscal-impuestos](https://www.fiscal-impuestos.com/real-decreto-ley-29-2026-medidas-tributarias-vivienda-irpf-iva-ibi-iivtnu-socimi-is); [Hispacolex](https://www.hispacolex.com/blog/civil-mercantil/real-decreto-ley-29-2026-novedades-fiscales-de-la-vivienda/); [notariasbarcelona](https://notariasbarcelona.es/real-decreto-ley-29-2026-novedades-fiscales/)
- **RDL 29/2026, other tax measures:**
  - A new 10% state IRPF deduction for tenants with a tax base below €33,007.20.
  - A **10% VAT rate for furnished lets of up to 30 nights**.
  - The 1.1% rate for imputed property income (imputación de rentas) is extended one year.
  - These measures need convalidation to survive.
  — [economistjurist](https://www.economistjurist.es/articulos-juridicos-destacados/medidas-tributarias-del-real-decreto-ley-29-2026-sobre-vivienda/); [fiscal-impuestos](https://www.fiscal-impuestos.com/real-decreto-ley-29-2026-medidas-tributarias-vivienda-irpf-iva-ibi-iivtnu-socimi-is)
- **The repeal of RDL 26/2026** on 2 Oct voided its amendments to the IRPF law, the civil procedure law (LEC) and the local finance law (Haciendas Locales). — [Gibobs](https://www.gibobs.com/blog/decretos-vivienda-2026-rechazados-tu-casa/); [WOLO](https://www.wolorent.com/blog/congreso-rechaza-ambos-decretos-ley-vivienda-2026)
- **VAT (general doctrine).** Letting housing is exempt unless the landlord provides hotel-type services, which then attract 10% VAT. Regular interior cleaning (for example weekly), weekly linen change or staffed reception count as hotel-type; cleaning only at check-in and check-out does not. DGT V0273-24 (29 Feb 2024): a let to individuals used exclusively as housing, with no hotel services, is exempt. — [search summary of net2rent / asesorexcelente / Primera Lectura](https://primeralecturaediciones.com/?p=20252467); [net2rent](https://net2rent.com/iva-en-alquiler-vacacional-la-guia-definitiva/)

### Inferences
- **Under the reduction regime in force until RDL 29/2026 applies:**
  - Room lets to students on academic-year contracts get **0% reduction**, so the full net income is taxed.
  - Whole-flat or room lets where students register as main residents (empadronamiento) on 12-month-plus main-residence contracts *may* get 50%, or more under the Ley 12/2023 tiers. That requires evidence of permanent residence, which students who return home in summer struggle to provide.
  - Background knowledge, not verified in this session: the Ley 12/2023 tiers in art. 23.2 LIRPF, from 2024, were 90% (stressed zone, rent cut by more than 5%), 70% (stressed zone, tenant aged 18–35, or first let), 60% (rehabilitation) and 50% (general).
- The RDL 29/2026 scale keeps the "vivienda" requirement. The DGT temporada exclusion therefore presumably continues, so student temporada income would still get no reduction.
- **VAT:** for temporada lets of more than 30 nights without hotel services, the exemption is expected to continue. Lets of up to 30 nights (e.g., summer-school stays) would carry 10% VAT under RDL 29/2026.
- **ITP/AJD (transfer tax) on purchase:** set by each autonomous community, roughly 6–11% for resale homes. Not researched here; see the purchase-cost notes.

### Gaps
- Could not verify in the BOE text the exact start date of the new IRPF scale (1 Dec 2026 vs 1 Jan 2027).
- No DGT ruling found yet on how RDL 29/2026 treats temporada or room lets.
- No 2025–2026 DGT ruling found specific to student rooms with cleaning services.

## 4. Contract type: LAU art. 3 (temporada / uso distinto de vivienda) vs vivienda habitual; recharacterisation risk

### Takeaway
Before Oct 2026, student lets were commonly signed as art. 3 "uso distinto de vivienda" (temporada) contracts, which carry no 5-year extension and no stressed-zone cap. The RDL 28/29/2026 reform makes recharacterisation automatic: if the cause is missing or not documented, or contracts are chained or exceed 12 months, the contract is treated as vivienda habitual from day one. Cataluña already applies the main-residence caps to temporada contracts.

### Cited Findings
- Without a real, documentable cause the contract is a main-residence lease "desde el primer día" (from day one). Studies are an accepted cause. Contracts exceeding 12 months without cause, or more than two consecutive contracts with the same tenant, convert to a 5-year (individual landlord) or 7-year (legal entity) main-residence lease. — [economistjurist](https://www.economistjurist.es/actualidad-juridica/legislacion/alquiler-de-temporada-asi-pasa-a-ser-vivienda-habitual-con-las-nuevas-reglas/); [legalitas](https://www.legalitas.com/actualidad/novedades-vivienda); [Roca Junyent](https://www.rocajunyent.com/es/eventos-y-publicaciones/articulos/nuevas-medidas-urgentes-vivienda-dos-reales-decretos-leyes)
- To convert a temporada contract, the landlord must give six months' notice and pay the tenant compensation at expiry, except in listed cases (one source; unverified). — [search summary of dig.es](https://www.dig.es/alquiler-de-temporada-habitaciones-2026/)
- Cataluña (Ley 11/2025): temporada and room contracts are subject to main-residence rent limits; only tourist or holiday contracts are excluded. — [idealista](https://www.idealista.com/news/inmobiliario/vivienda/2025/12/18/877252-cataluna-aprueba-regular-el-alquiler-de-temporada-y-de-habitaciones)
- The tax agency treats September–June student leases as temporada (DGT V0444-24). — [Jorge Borrajo](https://jorgeborrajo.com/alquiler-a-estudiantes-y-reduccion-en-el-irpf/)

### Inferences
- **There is a tension between the civil and tax tracks:**
  - Signing as *vivienda habitual* (a 12-month-plus contract with the student registered) may unlock the IRPF reduction. It also exposes the landlord to the 5- or 7-year extension, stressed-zone caps and eviction-procedure protections.
  - Signing as *temporada* keeps flexibility but gets no IRPF reduction, and now requires a documented study cause each year (enrolment certificate, home address elsewhere) and care with renewals.
- **Practical safeguards if the RDL survives:**
  - Separate contracts for each academic year.
  - A study cause backed by documents.
  - Avoid more than two consecutive contracts with the same student.
  - Avoid continuous occupancy beyond 12 months.

### Gaps
- No court rulings (Audiencias Provinciales) on recharacterisation under the new rules were found; it is too soon after 8 Oct 2026.
- Earlier case law on recharacterising student art. 3 contracts was not searched.

## 5. Licences, habitability certificate (cédula de habitabilidad), municipal limits on shared flats / coliving, residences

### Takeaway
Searches found no confirmed 2026 municipal ban on ordinary shared flats (pisos compartidos) let to students in the main university cities. Coliving is mainly regulated through urban planning:
- Madrid has been processing a planning amendment that defines "residencia compartida" (shared residence).
- Barcelona is targeting tourist flats (no licence renewals by 2028) and has struggled to stop irregular room or temporada "coliving".

Habitability certificates are a regional requirement and were not researched in depth.

### Cited Findings
- Madrid is processing an amendment (Modificación Puntual) to its general urban plan (PGOU) adding coliving as a residential use, "residencia compartida": private bedroom and bathroom, shared kitchen and laundry. Current status unclear; the article may date from 2023. — [LegalToday](https://www.legaltoday.com/practica-juridica/derecho-civil/civil/regulacion-del-coliving-ii-los-modelos-urbanisticos-de-barcelona-y-madrid-2023-06-01/)
- The Comunidad de Madrid announced it would drop the 90-day tourist-let limit and prohibit renting rooms *for tourist use*. The announcement is undated, so it is unconfirmed for 2026. — [Bolsamanía](https://www.bolsamania.com/noticias/politica/economia--comunidad-de-madrid-eliminara-el-limite-de-90-dias-en-pisos-turisticos-y-prohibira-alquilar-por-habitaciones--3151603.html)
- Barcelona plans not to renew 10,101 tourist-flat licences, so they return to residential use by Nov 2028. — [Diari de Tarragona](https://www.diaridetarragona.com/internacional/192474/barcelona-eliminara-todos-los-pisos-turisticos-en-cuatro-anos-ig20049661.html)
- Barcelona (Gràcia): flats divided into rooms and run by a temporary-rental company; councillors criticised weak enforcement against such coliving. — [Ara](https://en.ara.cat/society/barcelona-fails-to-stop-irregular-coliving-in-gracia-blog_1_5386175.html)

### Inferences
- **Tourist rules.** Lets of up to 30 nights or with hotel services can be classed as tourist accommodation, which requires a regional licence and is banned in many central areas (Barcelona, Madrid's central district, Valencia, Málaga, etc.). Keep student lets above 31 days and without hotel services.
- **Habitability certificate.** Cataluña, Valencia, Navarra and others require it to let a home (cédula / licencia de primera ocupación). Some regions have overcrowding or minimum-surface rules that can limit the number of rooms (e.g., Cataluña's Decreto 141/2012). This is background knowledge, not verified in this session.
- **Converting to a residence.** Turning a flat into a formal student residence or coliving building requires a change of use or a hospitality or residential licence. That is not viable for a single flat.

### Gaps
- No sources were retrieved on rules for Valencia, Salamanca, Granada or Sevilla.
- Not searched: occupancy and overcrowding limits per region, and community-of-owners (comunidad de propietarios) bans (art. 17.12 Ley de Propiedad Horizontal covers tourist lets only).

## 6. Announced measures for 2026–2027

### Takeaway
- **Plan Estatal de Vivienda 2026–2030:** approved (RD 326/2026 of 22 Apr 2026, in force 24 Apr 2026). It has a €7bn budget, funded 60% by the State and 40% by the regions. It includes youth rent aid, including aid for renting a room. Nothing specific on student residences was found.
- **Near-term:** the decisive events are the 12-Nov vote on RDL 28/29/2026 and the 29-Nov general election.

### Cited Findings
- RD 326/2026, Plan Estatal de Vivienda 2026–2030: published in the BOE on 23 Apr 2026, in force 24 Apr; €7,000m; State 60% and regions 40%. It is the first plan under Ley 12/2023. — [derecholocal](https://derecholocal.es/novedad-legislativa/aprobacion-del-plan-estatal-de-vivienda-2026-2030); [Primera Lectura](https://primeralecturaediciones.com/normas/archivo/real-decreto-326-2026-de-22-de-abril-por-el-que-se-regula-el-plan-estatal-de-vivienda-2026-2030/); [Brains RE](https://brainsre.news/asi-es-nuevo-plan-estatal-vivienda-2026-2030/)
- **Youth rent aid amounts conflict.** One source says €250/month for a whole home and €150 for a room; the draft decree said a maximum of €300 for a home and €200 for a room. Verify in the BOE. — [Rankia](https://www.rankia.com/blog/mejores-hipotecas/7257559-plan-estatal-vivienda-2026-2030); [Cronista](https://www.cronista.com/espana/economia-finanzas/alquileres-regalados-el-gobierno-cubre-hasta-el-60-del-alquiler-para-quienes-cumplan-estos-requisitos/)
- **Convalidation vote.** The vote on RDL 28/29/2026 is scheduled for 12 Nov 2026, the day after a general strike; the deadline is 13 Nov. The rent-extension decree could fall on a 34–34 tie if PNV votes no. — [La Región](https://www.laregion.es/espana/congreso-votara-decretos-vivienda-12-comision-permanente-huelga-general_1_20261016-4454547.html); [Moncloa.com](https://www.moncloa.com/2026/10/17/decretos-vivienda-congreso-12n-3450016)

### Inferences
- **Youth rent aid** (Bono Alquiler Joven and similar; room rentals eligible) may support students' ability to pay. Payment requires a formal contract and usually registration of residence (empadronamiento), which pushes towards main-residence-style contracts.
- **2027 outlook:** policy depends on the 29-Nov election.
  - A PP-led government would likely not extend the temporada and room caps, might reverse some RDL measures, and has said it opposes stressed-zone caps.
  - A continuation of the current coalition would likely keep or harden them.
  - Cataluña's Ley 11/2025 persists regardless, subject to the Constitutional Court.

### Gaps

- No information found on the platforms in the 29-Nov election (PP/Vox/PSOE/Sumar housing pledges).
