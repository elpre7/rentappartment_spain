# Nuevas universidades y nuevos campus en España (cursos 2023-24 a 2027-28): inventario por ciudad para la demanda de alojamiento estudiantil

> Research date: 18 Oct 2026. **Method caveat:** direct page fetching was blocked by the network proxy for nearly every press domain (eldiario.es, elespanol.com, infobae.com, lavozdelsur.es, ciencia.gob.es, etc.). All findings below come from search-engine result summaries and snippets of the cited pages, not from full-text reads. Figures should be spot-checked against the original before they go into a final investor document. Status tags used: **[CONFIRMADO]** = operating or officially authorised with a date; **[APROBADO-PENDIENTE]** = recognised by law but not yet teaching or without final authorisation; **[ANUNCIADO]** = project or plan only.

## 1. Universidades privadas reconocidas o autorizadas por las CCAA desde 2022: dónde, cuándo y con qué tamaño

### Takeaway
Since about 2022 the regions have recognised roughly 11+ new private universities. On union counts, Spain will have more private (51) than public (50) universities in 2026-27. For physical student housing demand, the most relevant new on-site projects are concentrated in **Málaga** (three new private universities, two of them on-site, from 2025-26), **Madrid** (UNIE campus for 5,000 students, Oct 2026), **Asturias** (Nebrija Avilés 2026-27, Universidad Europea Gijón 2027-28, Alfonso X Oviedo), **Badajoz** (UNINDE), **A Coruña** (Cesuga / "Universidad Emilia Pardo Bazán") and **Palma** (UMAC). Several of the others, including Utamed and Canarias' Universidad Tecnológica (TECH), are mainly online and generate little housing demand.

### Inferences
- The strongest "new on-site private university" signals for student housing are **Málaga** (two new on-site universities plus Utamed's urban site, all from 2025-26 and ramping up year by year), **Madrid** (UNIE, see section 2), **Gijón** (2027-28), **Avilés** (small: 60+30 students in year 1), **Oviedo**, **Badajoz** (UNINDE) and potentially **Palma** (UMAC) and **A Coruña** (Cesuga).
- New universities typically ramp up over 4-6 years. Under the now-annulled RD 905/2025 the target was 4,500 students by year 6, which gives a rough ceiling for a "full-size" new private university. First-year intakes are much smaller (e.g. Nebrija Avilés: about 90).
- The online-heavy entrants (Utamed, TECH Canarias, UNIAR, CEU Núñez de Balboa) add little local housing demand.

### Gaps
- Targeted student numbers and plazas for Universidad Europea Málaga, Alfonso X Mare Nostrum, UNINDE, UMAC, UE Gijón and Alfonso X Oviedo were not found (fetch blocked).
- No official consolidated list (RUCT) was retrieved. Whether UMAC and UNINDE actually began teaching in Sept/Oct 2026 is unverified.
- No information was found on new private universities in Comunidad Valenciana, Castilla y León, Cantabria, Navarra, País Vasco, La Rioja or Cataluña for 2023-27.

## 3. Estado del Real Decreto 905/2025 (reforma del RD 640/2021) y efecto sobre los proyectos privados

### Takeaway
RD 905/2025 (7 Oct 2025, BOE 8 Oct 2025) tightened the requirements for creating universities: at least 4,500 students within 6 years, 10 grados, 6 másteres and 3 doctorados, housing for 10% of students, and binding quality-agency reports. The **Tribunal Supremo annulled it in full** in a judgment dated **30 Sept 2026** (reported 8 Oct 2026), on procedural grounds: unjustified urgent processing that skipped public consultation. The appeal was brought by the Balearic Government. The original RD 640/2021 rules, with no minimum student count, apply again. This reopens the path for the roughly 15 pending projects. The government could re-issue the decree through the ordinary procedure.

### Cited Findings
- **Real Decreto 905/2025, de 7 de octubre** modifies RD 640/2021 (creation, recognition and authorisation of universities), RD 1509/2008 (RUCT), RD 1002/2010 and RD 822/2021. — [BOE-A-2025-20002](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2025-20002)
- Key requirements: a "proyecto global" that is academically and financially solid, with a critical mass of students in Grado, Máster and Doctorado. Quantitative minimums were 4,500 students, 10 grados, 6 másteres and 3 doctorados (vs 2 doctorados in 2021). Quality agencies' (ANECA or regional) reports become binding. — [CCSU](https://ccsu.es/puntos-clave-y-novedades-del-real-decreto-que-afecta-a-la-creacion-de-universidades/); [FSIE analysis](https://fsie.es/documentos/ficheros/COMUNICACION/Actualidad/2025/09_10_2025_RD_Univers_Privadas/ACTUALIDAD_JURIDICA_Real_Decreto_640_2021_Universidades_Privadas.pdf)
- The decree also required housing (alojamiento) for 10% of students; this requirement also falls with the annulment. — [Crónica del Henares, Oct 2026](https://www.cronicadelhenares.com/2026/10/supremo-anula-decreto-universidades-analisis.html)
- Critics argued the 4,500 threshold "radically prevents" small universities and that the decree was applied to projects already in process. — [Almacén de Derecho](https://almacendederecho.org/patologia-de-un-regimen-regulatorio-a-proposito-del-real-decreto-905-2025-y-su-aplicacion-a-las-universidades-privadas)
- **Annulment.** The Sala de lo Contencioso-Administrativo of the Supremo upheld the Balearic Government's appeal. The judgment is dated 30 Sept 2026. - After the annulment, the original RD 640/2021 applies, which sets no minimum student number. The merits of the reform remain open, and the government could re-process it. — [Sáez Abogados](https://www.saezabogados.com/real-decreto-905-2025-universidades/); [Crónica del Henares](https://www.cronicadelhenares.com/2026/10/supremo-anula-decreto-universidades-analisis.html)

### Inferences
- With RD 905/2025 gone, the pending projects should move faster through regional parliaments in late 2026 and 2027: Madrid (Felipe II Aranjuez, Universidad Abierta de Europa, TAI), Extremadura (UE Cáceres, CEU Núñez de Balboa, UAE Extremadura), Aragón (Power University, NCI Calatayud, UNIAR) and Galicia (Cesuga). The risk is a re-issued decree with proper consultation.
- The annulled 10% student-housing requirement would have pushed new private universities to build their own residences. Without it, more of their demand flows into the private rental and PBSA market.

## 4. Crecimiento de la matrícula (SIIU / Datos y cifras), ciudades de crecimiento rápido

### Takeaway
Total enrolment reached a record of about **1.9 million in 2025-26** (+21% in a decade). The private share almost doubled to **about 30%** (from 15%). Private Grado enrolment doubled and private Máster enrolment tripled (private now exceeds public in Máster), while public Grado fell 5%. City-level growth data was not retrievable.

### Cited Findings
- 2025-26: almost 1.9M students, a historic record, +21% vs a decade ago. Almost 30% are in private universities (vs 15% ten years ago). Private Grado students doubled while public fell 5%. Private Máster tripled and now exceeds public. — [Ministerio, Datos y Cifras del SUE 2025/26](https://www.ciencia.gob.es/dam/jcr:747444d8-7089-4b15-97e1-0fc27874e6a7/DatosCifras_SUE2025_2026.pdf); [Datos clave, 7 Oct 2025](https://www.ciencia.gob.es/dam/jcr:b70dd7bb-cbd2-4efb-b4fe-e69059986611/DatosClave_SIU.pdf)
- 2024-25 (EEU advance): 1,827,272 enrolled, of whom 1,330,892 were in public universities. Private universities hold 23% of Grado, 53% of Máster and 7% of Doctorado students. — [EEU 2024-25 avance](https://www.ciencia.gob.es/dam/jcr:08f45793-116d-4df2-8ddd-207662c3c6ee/PrincipalesResultadosEstudiantes2025.pdf)
- The EEU 2025-26 advance is listed for release in Oct 2026. — [Ministerio de Educación, últimas estadísticas](https://www.educacionfpydeportes.gob.es/servicios-al-ciudadano/estadisticas/ultimas.html)
- Fundación CYD publishes a regional perspective, "Las universidades españolas, una perspectiva autonómica 2025". — [Fundación CYD](https://www.fundacioncyd.org/la-fundacion-cyd-publica-las-universidades-espanolas-una-perspectiva-autonomica-2025-que-analiza-las-caracteristicas-del-sistema-universitario-de-cada-cc-aa/)
- Unofficial: Madrid (19.2%) and Cataluña (17.8%) hold the largest shares of students (commercial site, not verified against the Ministry). — [Tesify](https://tesify.es/universitarios-espana-estadisticas-sistema-universitario-2026)

### Inferences
- Private-sector growth is concentrated where private universities cluster: Madrid, Valencia, Barcelona, and now Málaga. New on-site private entrants (Málaga, Asturias, Badajoz, Palma) are where incremental out-of-town students, who need housing, will appear. Private Máster growth (often international, 1-year stays) supports demand for furnished short-to-medium lets in Madrid, Barcelona, Valencia and Málaga.
