## Q1. Idealista / Fotocasa rent €/m² and annual change by capital (2024–2026)

### Takeaway
Idealista puts the national asking rent at 15.1 €/m²/month in Sept 2026. That is +5.6% YoY, and −0.4% QoQ, the first quarterly fall since Q3 2021. In 2025 rents rose +8.5% (Dec 2025, 14.7 €/m²). Growth peaked at +10.9% YoY in Sep–Oct 2025. In 2026 it is decelerating in the big cities (Barcelona, Bilbao, San Sebastián, Vitoria and Girona flat or negative) and accelerating in mid-size and small capitals: Huesca, Huelva, Soria, Toledo, Zaragoza, Oviedo, Castellón and Salamanca. Fotocasa shows a softer trend: 14.10 €/m² in Sept 2026, +3% YoY, −4.7% QoQ.

### Inferences
- Since mid-2025 the fastest growth has moved from the big metros to mid-size and smaller capitals. In Aug–Sep 2026 the double-digit risers are Huesca, Huelva, Soria, Toledo, Zaragoza, Oviedo and Castellón. Large Basque and Catalan cities are flat or falling. This fits with regulated "zonas tensionadas" (Catalonia; the Basque Country) and the July 2026 idealista exclusion of seasonal listings, which hits large cities hardest.
- Among the student-heavy mid-size cities, Zaragoza (+11.1%), Oviedo (+11%), Valladolid (+11.1% in 2025), Salamanca (+7.6–8%), Vigo (+7.9%) and Castellón (+10.9%) are clearly above the national +5.6–5.8%. Santiago (+1.6%), A Coruña (+3.2%) and Granada (+4.7% in 2025) are below it.
- Fotocasa's Valencia +16.5% and idealista's Valencia +3.5% are irreconcilable without seeing both pages. The idealista figure reflects post-July-2026 methodology, so treat Valencia's growth as uncertain.

## Q2. Room prices in shared flats (habitaciones) by city: idealista, HousingAnywhere and others

### Takeaway
Room prices have nearly stalled while supply surged. Idealista's national room price went from about 400 € (Q1–Q2 2024) to 410 € (Q4 2024), 405 € (Q3 2025), 425 € (2025 close, +4%), 430 € (Q1 2026, +2%) and 425 € (Q2 2026, +1%). Room supply grew between 12% and 43% YoY each quarter. Barcelona (~600 €) and Madrid (550–587 €) top the table. Typical mid-size university cities sit around 280–400 €. HousingAnywhere's 2026/27 academic-year data show Salamanca at 370 € (+2.9%) and Granada at 333 € (−2.9%).

## Q3. Official data: SERPAVI (Ministerio de Vivienda) and INE

### Takeaway
Official municipal data lag by about two years. SERPAVI 2026 (1st ed. March 2026, revised July 2026) publishes 2024 rents (tax-return based) for municipalities over 25,000 inhabitants. INE's experimental IPVA published 2024 data in May 2026. INE's monthly series (CPI rent, IRAV) are national only and show much lower growth than the portals.

### Cited Findings
- SERPAVI 2026: Ministerio de Vivienda y Agenda Urbana, DG Vivienda y Suelo. 1st edition March 2026, revised July 2026. Tables give average rent €/m² and average amounts for main municipalities over 25,000 inhabitants, referring to 2024. Viewers are available at census section, district, municipality, province and region level — [MIVAU publication](https://publicaciones.transportes.gob.es/serpavi-2026-sistema-estatal-de-referencia-del-precio-del-alquiler-de-vivienda); [MIVAU SERPAVI page](https://www.mivau.gob.es/vivienda/alquila-bien-es-tu-derecho/serpavi); [Methodology PDF](https://cdn.mivau.gob.es/portal-web-mivau/vivienda/serpavi/2026-03-18_Metodologia_SERPAVI.pdf)
- Reference index system updated by Resolution of 16 Apr 2026 (BOE-A-2026-8691) — [BOE](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-8691)
- The Basque Country and Navarra are not covered (foral tax regimes). Catalonia declared 140 municipalities as tensioned areas — [Housfy](https://housfy.com/blog/ya-se-puede-consultar-el-indice-estatal-de-precios-para-limitar-el-alquiler/)
- Example SERPAVI value: A Coruña 7.10 €/m² (median 537 €/month), 2024 data — [precioalquiler.com](https://precioalquiler.com/alquiler/coruna-a) (secondary aggregator). Compare idealista asking price of 11.4 €/m² in Sep 2026.
- INE IPVA (experimental): 2024 data published May 2026. OCU says the published information does not break down by city — [OCU, May 2026](https://www.ocu.org/fincas-y-casas/gestion/alquiler/analisis/2026/05/estadistica-precios-alquiler). Caveat: INE's IPVA has historically included municipal tables for larger municipalities, so OCU's statement may refer to a limited release. Verify on ine.es.
- INE CPI rent component: +2.6% YoY in Sept 2026, the fifth consecutive monthly rise — [El Diario de Madrid, 14 Oct 2026](https://www.eldiariodemadrid.es/articulo/vivienda/precio-alquiler-espana-septiembre-2026-subida-26-ciento-ine/20261014101157144093.html)
- IRAV (index for updating contracts signed after 26 May 2023): 2.54% in Sept 2026, its highest value, published 14 Oct 2026 — [Infobae](https://www.infobae.com/espana/agencias/2026/10/14/el-indice-para-actualizar-alquileres-sube-al-254-en-septiembre-y-marca-su-valor-mas-alto/); [INE IRAV](https://www.ine.es/uc/oC7D0Ncd); Jan 2026 value 2.14% — [El Boletín](https://www.elboletin.com/el-indice-del-ine-para-actualizar-el-alquiler-baja-al-214-en-enero-de-2026/)

### Inferences
- There is a large gap between asking prices for new lets (idealista and Fotocasa, +3% to +11%) and the stock of existing contracts (CPI rent +2.6%, IRAV-capped updates). New tenants, including students arriving each September, face the portal rates, not the CPI rate.
- SERPAVI 2024 contract-based €/m² values are typically 30–40% below current asking prices. Example: A Coruña at 7.1 vs 11.4 €/m².

### Gaps

## Q4. Which cities show the fastest rent growth, especially student-heavy ones?

### Takeaway
In 2026, the fastest whole-flat rent growth is in mid-size and small capitals. Among university cities, Zaragoza (+11.1%), Oviedo (+11%), Castellón (+10.9%), Huelva (+14.6%), Valladolid (+11.1% in 2025), Salamanca (~+8%) and Vigo (+7.9%) stand out. Big cities (Barcelona, Bilbao, San Sebastián, Girona, Lleida) are flat or falling. Room prices grow much more slowly everywhere (about 0–4%). The exception is the HousingAnywhere furnished segment in Barcelona (+8.3%).

### Cited Findings
- Aug 2026 top YoY risers: Huesca +15%, Huelva +14.6%, Soria +12.4%, Toledo +11.8%, Zaragoza +11.1%, Oviedo +11% (Castellón +10.9% also reported). Only San Sebastián, Vitoria, Girona and Bilbao fell — [idealista, 1 Sep 2026](https://www.idealista.com/news/inmobiliario/vivienda/2026/09/01/911881-el-precio-del-alquiler-sube-un-5-8-en-agosto-y-sigue-por-encima-de-los-15-euros-m2); [Forbes España](https://forbes.es/ultima-hora/1008725/el-precio-del-alquiler-en-espana-desacelera-su-subida-en-agosto-al-58-segun-idealista/)
- Sept 2026: Huesca +17.2%. Palma, Madrid, Málaga, Sevilla and Valencia rise most among large cities. Vitoria, San Sebastián, Barcelona, Lleida and Bilbao are below a year earlier — [idealista, 1 Oct 2026](https://www.idealista.com/news/inmobiliario/vivienda/2026/10/01/916616-el-precio-del-alquiler-sube-un-5-6-anual-en-septiembre-pero-cae-en-el-ultimo)

- Competition: people interested per rental home +16% YoY (Oct 2026) — [idealista, 15 Oct 2026](https://www.idealista.com/news/inmobiliario/vivienda/2026/10/15/918818-los-interesados-que-compiten-por-cada-vivienda-en-alquiler-crecen-un-16-en-el)
- Rooms: HousingAnywhere Q3 2025 fastest risers Valencia +6.3%, Zaragoza +4.2%. Q2 2026: Barcelona +8.3%, Madrid +3.4%, Valencia +2.4% — [HousingAnywhere](https://housinganywhere.com/spanish-room-rent-index-q3-2025); [que.es](https://www.que.es/2026/07/13/alquilar-habitacion-espana-2026-precios/)

### Inferences
- The student cities facing the most pressure on flats in 2026 are Zaragoza, Oviedo, Valladolid, Salamanca, Castellón, Huelva and Vigo, all with high single- or double-digit growth. In these cities, rising flat rents feed through to room rents with a lag. But room-supply growth (for example León +43%) is so far holding room prices down.
- Granada, Santiago and Sevilla show weaker growth in both flats and rooms. Granada's rooms are even falling per HousingAnywhere, so they are relatively less pressured.
