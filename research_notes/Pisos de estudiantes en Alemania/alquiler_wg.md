# WG room prices and residential asking rents in German university cities, 2023-2026

Method note: direct page fetching was blocked in this environment (proxy 403 / DNS failure for moses-mendelssohn-institut.de, mlp-se.de, empirica-institut.de, immowelt CDN). All figures below come from search-engine extracts of the primary press releases and of press coverage. The primary PDFs (MMI city tables, MLP/IW full table, empirica Preisspiegel, immowelt Mietkompass) could not be opened in full. Treat single city numbers as "reported by" the cited page; verify against the original PDF before publishing. Three different WG-price series exist and they are NOT comparable with each other:
1. **MMI / WG-Gesucht** (Moses Mendelssohn Institut): ~88-90 Hochschulstandorte; WG rooms in 2-3-person flats; warm rent; national "weighted average", city values reported as median; published each March (SoSe) and September (WiSe).
2. **empirica Preisspiegel WG-Mieten**: >120 Hochschulstädte; "Standardpreis"/mean asking rent for an unfurnished 10-30 m² room incl. Nebenkosten (warm).
3. **MLP Studentenwohnreport (IW Köln)**: 38 locations; hypothetical 20 m² "Muster-WG-Zimmer" and 30 m² "Musterwohnung", calculated warm rents from H1 listings; plus a quality- and location-adjusted price index (Studentenwohnpreisindex).

## 1. MMI / WG-Gesucht "Studentisches Wohnen" reports: average WG room prices and changes

### Takeaway
The MMI national average WG room rent rose from about 472 € (WiSe 2023/24, implied) to 489 € (WiSe 2024/25), 505 € (WiSe 2025/26, first time above 500 €), 512 € (SoSe 2026) and 510 € (WiSe 2026/27). Growth slowed sharply to about +1% YoY in autumn 2026. München is by far the most expensive (median 850 € in WiSe 2026/27), followed by Berlin and Hamburg (650 €) and Köln (640 €). Chemnitz is the cheapest at about 305 €. City-level tables for the other ~85 cities were not accessible.

- München gained about 100 € (+13%) between WiSe 2023/24 (750) and WiSe 2026/27 (850). That makes it the clearest "fastest riser" in absolute terms among cities with reported figures.

## 2. MLP Studentenwohnreport (IW Köln) 2024-2026 by city

### Takeaway
MLP/IW 2026 (published 30.09.2026, data H1 2026, 38 cities): the 30 m² Musterwohnung averages 567 € warm and the 20 m² Muster-WG-Zimmer 513 €. The quality-adjusted index rose +2.6% YoY (2025: +2.3%), with a 3-year average of +4.0%/yr. München is the most expensive (~880 €) and Chemnitz the cheapest (~280 €). The fastest 3-year risers are Hamburg and Saarbrücken (+5.8%/yr) and Bochum (+5.5%/yr). In the 2025 report the leaders were Leipzig, Freiburg and Konstanz (>6%/yr). Leipzig fell −2.2% in 2026.

## 3. Asking rents €/m² (Angebotsmieten) for flats and YoY change by city

### Takeaway
General asking rents are still rising, but more slowly. immowelt (01.10.2026, existing flats) puts Germany at 10.83 €/m² (+2.1% YTD vs +3.9% a year earlier), München at 21.65 €/m² (+2.8% YTD), Berlin at 14.63 €/m² (+1.8%) and Leipzig at 9.59 €/m² (+1.3%, the lowest of the 15 largest cities). GREIX Q3 2026 shows +3.7% YoY nationally, with Leipzig and Stuttgart at +1.9% and Berlin (+2.1% QoQ) and Düsseldorf above average. The city-by-city €/m² for the mid-sized university cities on the list was not found.

## 4. Furnished rooms/micro-apartments vs unfurnished; other WG series (empirica)

## 5. BAföG Wohnkostenpauschale as an affordability reference

### Takeaway
The pauschale has been 380 €/month since WiSe 2024/25. A cabinet bill (12.08.2026) would raise it to 440 € from SoSe 2027 (1 April 2027), delayed from WiSe 2026/27, but it is not yet law. 380 € covers the MLP Musterwohnung only in Chemnitz (2026). 440 € would cover it in only three of the 38 cities. Against the ~510 € MMI average WG room, the gap is ~130 €.

### Cited Findings
