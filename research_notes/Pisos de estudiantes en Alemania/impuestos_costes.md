# Taxes, transaction costs and financing: private German tax resident buying an existing flat to rent to students (as of October 2026)

Not tax advice. Status date: 18 Oct 2026. Note on sources: gesetze-im-internet.de and hausundgrund.de could not be fetched from the research environment (DNS failure), so statute wording is taken from secondary sources or, where marked "general knowledge, verify", from the researcher's background knowledge (cutoff mid-2026). The report writer should keep those flags.

## 1. Transaction costs: Grunderwerbsteuer, Notar/Grundbuch, Maklerprovision

### Takeaway
Grunderwerbsteuer in 2026 runs from 3.5% (Bayern) to 6.5% (BB, NRW, SL, SH). The changes from 2023 to 2025 were: Sachsen and Hamburg up to 5.5% (2023), Thüringen down to 5.0% (2024) and Bremen up to 5.5% (1 Jul 2025). No changes for 2026 were found. Total purchase costs come to about 5.0–12% of the price, depending on the Land and whether a broker is involved.

### Cited Findings
- **Rates in 2026.** Bayern 3.5%. BW, NI, RP, ST, TH 5.0%. BE, HE, MV 6.0%. HH, SN 5.5%. BB, NRW, SL, SH 6.5%. Sources: [capitalo.de](https://www.capitalo.de/baufinanzierung/grunderwerbsteuer), [rechner-hub.de Bundesländervergleich 2026](https://rechner-hub.de/grunderwerbsteuer/bundeslaender-vergleich/), [baufi24.de](https://www.baufi24.de/grunderwerbsteuer/). Overview: [PwC](https://www.pwc.de/de/steuerberatung/die-grunderwerbsteuersaetze-in-deutschland-im-ueberblick.html).
- **Bremen** went from 5.0% to 5.5% for acquisitions realised from 1 Jul 2025 (Senate bill Drs. 21/885, 3 Dec 2024). The date of the purchase contract decides which rate applies. Sources: [service.bremen.de](https://www.service.bremen.de/dienstleistungen/grunderwerbsteuer-9673), [Bremische Bürgerschaft Drs. 21/885](https://www.bremische-buergerschaft.de/dokumente/wp21/land/drucksache/D21L0885.pdf). baufi24.de still lists 5.0%, which is out of date.
- The Bremen bill gave the national average rate at the time as 5.53% ([Drs. 21/885](https://www.bremische-buergerschaft.de/dokumente/wp21/land/drucksache/D21L0885.pdf)). Haus & Grund Bremen worked out an example: €22,000 tax on a €400k price at 5.5% ([Haus & Grund Bremen](https://www.hausundgrund.de/verband/bremen/haus-grund-erhoehung-der-grunderwerbsteuer-durch-rotrotgruen-fatal-fuer-land-bremen)).
- **Thüringen** cut its rate from 6.5% to 5.0% on 1 Jan 2024. One page dates the cut to 2026, but the majority of sources say 2024 ([smart-rechner Thüringen](https://www.smart-rechner.de/grunderwerbsteuer/ratgeber/grunderwerbsteuer_in_thueringen.php), [mehrwertsteuerrechner.de](https://www.mehrwertsteuerrechner.de/grunderwerbsteuer)).
- **Sachsen** went from 3.5% to 5.5% in 2023 ([smart-rechner Sachsen](https://www.smart-rechner.de/grunderwerbsteuer/ratgeber/grunderwerbsteuer_in_sachsen.php)). **Hamburg** went from 4.5% to 5.5% on 1 Jan 2023 ([mehrwertsteuerrechner.de](https://www.mehrwertsteuerrechner.de/grunderwerbsteuer)).
- One comparison site says no further changes are planned for 2026 ([rechner-hub.de Bremen](https://rechner-hub.de/grunderwerbsteuer/bremen/)).
- A Grunderwerbsteuer reform at federal level (share deals, keeping the extended trade-tax deduction) is being discussed in lobbying filings. Nothing is enacted ([Lobbyregister RV0018228](https://www.lobbyregister.bundestag.de/media/6e/59/807155/Lobbyregister-Regelungsvorhaben-Detailansicht-RV0018228-2026-07-07_09-13-04.pdf)).
- **Maklerprovision.** Under §§ 656a–656d BGB (in force since 23 Dec 2020), the halving rule applies to purchases of flats (Wohnungen) and single-family houses by consumers. If the broker acts for both sides, buyer and seller must pay the same amount, and a contract that breaks this rule is void (LG München II, 30 Jan 2023, 2 O 4028/21). In 2026 the BGH clarified what counts as an "Einfamilienhaus" in a case where the broker had charged 3.57%. Sources: [Görg, 10 Jul 2026](https://www.goerg.de/de/aktuelles/veroeffentlichungen/10-07-2026/halbteilungsgebot-im-maklerrecht-bgh-konkretisiert-den-begriff-des-einfamilienhauses), [otto-schmidt 16 Jul 2026](https://www.otto-schmidt.de/news/zivil-und-zivilverfahrensrecht/zum-grundsatz-der-halftigen-teilung-der-maklerprovision-zwischen-verkaufer-und-kaufer-beim-erwerb-eines-einfamilienhauses-2026-07-16.html), [rechtsanwalt-krau.de](https://rechtsanwalt-krau.de/was-regelt-paragraf-656c-bgb-beim-immobilienkauf/).
- 3.57% (3% plus 19% VAT) per side is commonly quoted as the typical share each party pays ([sum.money Maklerprovisionsrechner](https://sum.money/de/maklerprovisionrechner/), a commercial source).

### Inferences
- The protection depends on the buyer being a consumer (Verbraucher). A private individual buying one flat as an investment is generally still a consumer, but no source addressed investment flats specifically. Regional practice also varies: in parts of eastern Germany the seller often pays the whole commission, so the buyer pays 0%.
- Notar plus Grundbuch: the brief's figure of about 1.5–2% of the price is the market convention. Rough split: notary about 1.0–1.5%, Grundbuch about 0.5% (fixed fee schedule under GNotKG). This is general knowledge and was not sourced in this round.
- Model inputs for total purchase costs: GrESt (by Land) + 1.5–2.0% notary/Grundbuch + 0–3.57% broker. Examples: Bayern without a broker ≈ 5%. NRW with a broker ≈ 12.1%.
- Purchase costs are not deductible immediately. They are capitalised pro rata into land (not depreciable) and building (depreciated) (general knowledge, verify).

### Gaps
- No primary statute text was fetched for any Land. Before relying on a rate, check the Land's GrESt-Satz law, especially for planned 2027 changes.
- No authoritative source was found for the notary/Grundbuch percentage.

## 2. Income tax on rental income (Einkünfte aus Vermietung und Verpachtung, § 21 EStG)

### Takeaway
Net rental income is taxed at the investor's personal marginal rate (up to 42–45% plus Soli/church tax). A Bestand flat gets straight-line AfA on the building share: 2% per year, or 2.5% if the building was finished before 1925, or 3% if finished after 2022. The 5% degressive AfA and the §7b Sonder-AfA only apply to new builds bought by the end of the completion year. Renovation in the first 3 years above 15% of the building cost is capitalised rather than deducted.

### Cited Findings
- **Degressive AfA (§ 7 Abs. 5a EStG).** 5% of the residual value for residential buildings whose construction started after 30 Sep 2023 and before 1 Oct 2029 (the date of the construction start notice counts). A buyer qualifies only if the purchase is completed by the end of the year of completion. For Bestand purchases it is not available. The owner may switch to straight-line at any time. Sources: [finanz.de AfA](https://www.finanz.de/steuern/afa/), [Kreditwesen/Creydt 2025 PDF](https://www.kreditwesen.de/mimas/system/files/content/inserts/2025/if_2025-06_dsd_creydt.pdf). One source gives "6%", which appears to be an error.
- **Sonder-AfA § 7b.** An extra 5% per year for 4 years, which can be combined with the degressive AfA (year 1: 5% + 5% = 10%). It requires EH40/QNG standard, a construction cost cap that was raised to €5,200/m², and an assessment base capped at €4,000/m². Sources: [Kreditwesen/Creydt](https://www.kreditwesen.de/mimas/system/files/content/inserts/2025/if_2025-06_dsd_creydt.pdf), [deutschland-rechner AfA](https://www.deutschland-rechner.de/afa-rechner). An existing student flat will practically never qualify.
- **15% rule (anschaffungsnahe Herstellungskosten, § 6 Abs. 1 Nr. 1a EStG).** If repair and modernisation costs within 3 years of purchase exceed 15% (net of VAT) of the building's acquisition cost, they are capitalised and depreciated instead of deducted. Recurring annual maintenance is excluded. If the threshold is crossed in year 3, earlier tax assessments can be amended (§ 175 AO). Sources: [steuer-berater.de](https://www.steuer-berater.de/lexikon/gebaeude), [Haufe](https://www.haufe.de/steuern/finanzverwaltung/erhaltungsaufwand-anschaffungsnahe-herstellungskosten-und-akhk/anschaffungsnahe-herstellungskosten-bei-gebaeuden_164_676758.html), [steuertipps.de](https://www.steuertipps.de/lexikon/a/anschaffungsnaher-herstellungsaufwand). A BMF letter dated January 2026 on separating repair costs from acquisition/construction costs exists ([IVD PDF](https://ivd.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/2026-01-26-instandsetzung-modernisierung-gebaeude-1.pdf)), but its content was not reviewed.
- **Purchase price split (Gebäude vs Grundstück).** The price is split by the ratio of market values. The BMF Arbeitshilfe (an Excel tool) is used as a plausibility check ([steuer-berater.de](https://www.steuer-berater.de/lexikon/gebaeude)).
- **Furnished rentals.** BFH IX R 14/17 (2018) governs how the local market rent (ortsübliche Miete) is set for furnished flats, including a furniture surcharge (Möblierungszuschlag) taken from the Mietspiegel where possible ([IWW](https://www.iww.de/ah/steuern/vermietung-bfh-so-ist-die-ortsuebliche-marktmiete-bei-moeblierter-wohnung-zu-ermitteln-f115578)).

### Inferences (general knowledge, verify against § 7 EStG)
- AfA rates under § 7 Abs. 4 EStG: 2% for buildings finished 1925–2022 (50 years), 2.5% for buildings finished before 1 Jan 1925 (40 years), 3% for buildings finished after 31 Dec 2022. A shorter actual remaining useful life can be shown with an expert report (BFH case law, BMF letter 2023).
- The building share is typically 70–85% in mid-sized cities and lower in expensive inner-city locations (e.g. Munich, where land is a large share of value). This drives the effective depreciation rate.
- Deductible costs (Werbungskosten): mortgage interest (not principal repayment), maintenance (Erhaltungsaufwand), the non-recoverable part of the Hausgeld including the WEG administrator fee, landlord insurance, broker fees for finding tenants, tax adviser costs, travel to the property, and Grundsteuer if not passed on to tenants. Payments into the WEG Instandhaltungsrücklage are deductible only when the reserve is actually spent (BFH), not when paid in.
- Furniture: depreciate over its useful life, typically about 10–13 years in the AfA tables. Assets up to €800 net (GWG) can be expensed immediately. Furniture income counts as V+V as long as the activity is not commercial.
- Loss offsetting (Verlustverrechnung): a V+V loss (common in the early years because of interest and AfA) offsets other income such as salary in the same year, with loss carryback/carryforward under § 10d. This requires an intention to make a profit (Einkünfteerzielungsabsicht), which is presumed for unlimited long-term residential lets.

### Gaps
- The § 7 EStG text could not be fetched. The 2%/2.5%/3% rates and dates above come from background knowledge.
- No 2026 source was found on whether the §7b caps or the 2029 deadline changed again in 2026.

## 3. Spekulationsfrist (§ 23 EStG): 10-year rule and reform proposals

### Takeaway
In force: a private seller's gain is tax-free after a holding period of more than 10 years. Within 10 years it is taxed at the personal rate, with depreciation already claimed added back to the gain. Proposed, not law: Bündnis 90/Die Grünen bill 21/6637 (23 Jun 2026) would abolish the holding period. Haus & Grund also reports (15 Oct 2026) that a Bundesrat finance committee recommendation supports abolition, possibly including properties whose period has not yet run out.

### Cited Findings
- The Green bill 21/6637 (filed 23 Jun 2026) would delete the 10-year period in § 23 EStG without replacement. The sponsors estimate about €6 bn in revenue. A similar motion was rejected on 26 Jun 2025 with votes from CDU/CSU, SPD and AfD. The coalition agreement does not mention the topic. Sources: [juhn.com](https://www.juhn.com/fachwissen/besteuerung-immobilien/spekulationsfrist-immobilien-abschaffen/), [rosepartner.de](https://www.rosepartner.de/blog/wird-die-steuerfreiheit-nach-ablauf-der-spekulationsfrist-abgeschafft.html), [reduco.ai, Aug 2026: "Nein – Stand August 2026"](https://reduco.ai/blog/immobilienverkauf/spekulationsfrist-abschaffung).
- Haus & Grund ("Länder planen Ewigkeitssteuer auf Immobilien", Oct 2026) criticises a Bundesrat finance committee recommendation to abolish the period, which could also hit existing holdings ([Haus & Grund](https://www.hausundgrund.de/laender-planen-ewigkeitssteuer-auf-immobilien), [Haus & Grund MV](https://www.hausundgrund.de/verband/mecklenburg-vorpommern/wenn-gerechtigkeit-zur-enteignung-auf-raten-wird)). The page could not be fetched, so this is known only from a search summary.
- **Context.** Jahressteuergesetz 2026: Bundesrat opinion on 25 Sep 2026, federal government's reply on 30 Sep 2026 (Drs. 21/8283), Bundestag first reading on 8 Oct 2026, final vote expected 13 Nov 2026. None of the summaries found lists abolition of the Spekulationsfrist among the JStG measures ([mtrlegal](https://www.mtrlegal.com/jstg-2026-bundesregierung-aeussert-sich-zum-bundesrat/), [Ebner Stolz](https://www.ebnerstolz.de/de/unser-angebot/leistungen/steuerberatung/allgemeine-steuerberatung/stellungnahme-bundesrat-jstg-2026-113679.html)).

### Inferences
- Abolition is politically unlikely in the current Bundestag, where CDU/CSU and SPD rejected it in 2025. It is still a real tail risk worth a scenario in the model (sale after more than 10 years taxed at the marginal rate on the gain, including the AfA claimed).
- A vermögensverwaltende GmbH never benefits from the 10-year exemption (general knowledge).

### Gaps
- The primary Bundesrat committee document was not found, so it is unclear whether the recommendation relates to the JStG 2026 or to another bill.

## 4. Renting to one's own student child (Elternwohnung / Kinder-WG) and § 21 Abs. 2 EStG

### Takeaway
Since 2021, if the rent is at least 66% of the local market rent, all costs are fully deductible. Between 50% and 66%, full deduction depends on a positive long-term profit forecast. Below 50%, costs are deductible only pro rata. The lease must pass an arm's-length test: written contract, actual payments by bank transfer, terms customary between strangers.

### Cited Findings
- 66% or more: full deduction. 50% to under 66%: profit-forecast test (positive forecast gives full deduction, negative gives pro rata). Under 50%: pro rata. These rules apply only to residential use, from 1 Jan 2021. Sources: [steuergo](https://www.steuergo.de/en/texte/2024/270/przychody_z_najmu_od_krewnych), [Haufe: Nahe Angehörige als Mieter](https://www.haufe.de/steuern/steuerwissen-tipps/nahe-angehoerige-als-mieter_170_347884.html).
- A lease to a dependent child can be recognised even if the child pays the rent from parental maintenance (Unterhalt), as long as the contract is valid under civil law and is carried out as agreed. Practical requirements: a written open-ended lease, traceable payments and rent of at least 66% of the local level ([verbandsbuero.de](https://www.verbandsbuero.de/steuervorteile-nutzen-so-klappts-bei-der-familienvermietung/), [Haufe](https://www.haufe.de/steuern/steuerwissen-tipps/nahe-angehoerige-als-mieter_170_347884.html)).
- For furnished flats, the 66% comparison includes the furniture surcharge (BFH IX R 14/17) ([IWW](https://www.iww.de/ah/steuern/vermietung-bfh-so-ist-die-ortsuebliche-marktmiete-bei-moeblierter-wohnung-zu-ermitteln-f115578)).
- Market context on parents buying flats for student children: [BHW press note, Jan 2025](https://www.bhw.de/dam/bhwde/mediendienst/2025.01.21_Wohneigentum_statt_Wohngemeinschaft.pdf).

### Inferences
- Common model: the parent buys a 2–3-room flat, rents one room to their own child and the other rooms to fellow students (Kinder-WG) at market rent. That keeps a profit forecast credible and supports the AfA and interest deductions.
- Interaction with Kindergeld/Kinderfreibetrag and Unterhalt deductions should be checked: rent paid by the child out of parental maintenance does not change Kindergeld eligibility (general knowledge, verify).

### Gaps
- The specific BFH decision on student children paying rent from Unterhalt was not verified from a primary source. The decision commonly cited is BFH IX R 30/98 from 2000 (general knowledge, verify).

## 5. Commercial status (Gewerblichkeit, trade tax) and VAT (Umsatzsteuer)

### Takeaway
Renting furnished rooms is normally asset management, not a business. Ordinary side services such as cleaning do not change that. Risk arises once the operation resembles a hotel, or under a packaged "all-in" concept aimed at students with standardised, separately paid extra services; the Hamburg tax authority has said so. For VAT, residential lets of more than 6 months are exempt (§ 4 Nr. 12 UStG). Accommodation of up to 6 months is taxable at 7% (§ 12 Abs. 2 Nr. 11 UStG), with extras at 19%, unless the small-business exemption applies.

### Cited Findings
- Renting or subletting furnished rooms is not commercial, even with cleaning or breakfast as side services. It becomes commercial when hotel-like organisation outweighs the use of the asset (BFH I R 182/79) ([NWB Experten-Blog](https://www.nwb-experten-blog.de/vermieter-als-gewerbetreibender), [lexetius](https://lexetius.com/1984,137)).
- The Hamburg tax authority (Fachinformation 1/2018, agreed with federal and Land authorities) treats it as commercial when the landlord offers a predefined overall concept to target groups such as students, young professionals or commuters, with standardised, separately paid extras from external providers that go beyond usual side services ([NWB Experten-Blog](https://www.nwb-experten-blog.de/vermieter-als-gewerbetreibender)).
- **VAT.** § 4 Nr. 12 S. 2 UStG excludes short-term accommodation from the exemption. The 7% reduced rate applies to rooms held for short-term accommodation (§ 12 Abs. 2 Nr. 11). Up to about 6 months counts as short-term, judged by the landlord's intention as shown by objective circumstances (FG Saarland, 6 Feb 2002). Meals, internet and TV are taxed at 19%. Sources: [IWW Quellenmaterial](https://www.iww.de/lgp/quellenmaterial/id/234117), [Deubner: FG Saarland](https://www.deubner-steuern.de/produkte/kanzleitrainer/doc/vermietung-von-raeumen-in-einem-aussiedlerwohnheim-umsatzsteuer-1992-493971), [steuerschroeder.de](https://www.steuerschroeder.de/steuerlexikon/3924375).

### Inferences
- Practical rule: give students leases of at least 12 months or open-ended leases (not single semesters of under 6 months). Keep services to furniture, internet and normal common-area cleaning. Bill internet and utilities as part of an all-inclusive rent, not as a sold service package. This keeps the let VAT-free and outside trade tax.
- If trade tax did apply, there is an allowance of €24,500 profit for individuals, and trade tax is largely credited against income tax (§ 35 EStG). The bigger costs are losing the 10-year exemption (the property becomes business property, so any sale gain is taxed) and the bookkeeping burden (general knowledge).
- Small-business VAT threshold since 1 Jan 2025: €25,000 prior-year / €100,000 current-year turnover. Exempt long-term rents do not count toward it (general knowledge, verify; one source still cites €22,000, which is outdated).

### Gaps
- The full Hamburg Fachinformation 1/2018 text was not reviewed. No 2026 BFH ruling specific to student flats was found.

## 6. Financing (rates, equity, KfW, GmbH)

### Takeaway
In October 2026, 10-year fixed mortgage rates are about 4.0–4.2% effective for top borrowers, after rising from about 3.75% in September. KfW has no purchase programme for investors buying Bestand. Only the KfW 261/262 renovation loans are open to landlords. A vermögensverwaltende GmbH rarely pays off for one or a few flats.

### Cited Findings
- Dr. Klein: "Top-Bauzins" from 4.16% effective with 10-year fixed rate (as of 13 Oct 2026). 15–30-year fixes cost about 4.3–4.7%. The top rate rose from about 3.75% to 4.25% in September 2026, a high for the year, and is expected to range between 4.0% and 4.5% ([Dr. Klein Darlehenszinsen](https://www.drklein.de/finanzlexikon/darlehenszinsen.html), [Dr. Klein Zinsentwicklung](https://www.drklein.de/negativzinsen-baufinanzierung-kredite.html)).
- Schwäbisch Hall (27 Sep 2026): 10-year rates are back above 4.0%, with no sustained fall expected ([Schwäbisch Hall](https://www.schwaebisch-hall.de/baufinanzierung/bauzinsen-aktuell.html?source=11)).
- **KfW 261** (Wohngebäude-Kredit for full energy renovation to Efficiency House standard, or buying a freshly renovated Efficiency House) is open to investors, with repayment grants of up to about 45% at the top level. Sources report a smaller 2026 budget. **KfW 262** covers single measures, up to €60k per unit. **KfW 308 "Jung kauft Alt"** is for owner-occupying families, so not for investors. Sources: [foerder-welt.de](https://www.foerder-welt.de/content/foerderwelt/de/wohnen/sanieren/sanieren-und-vermieten.html), [enpal KfW 261](https://www.enpal.de/waermepumpe/kfw-261), [reduco.ai KfW 261 2026](https://reduco.ai/blog/foerderung/kfw-261-kredit-komplettsanierung) (commercial sources, terms vary).
- 2026 lending market focus is shifting to existing stock ([cash-online](https://www.cash-online.de/a/baufinanzierung-2026-der-bestand-rueckt-in-den-fokus-725879/)).

### Inferences (general knowledge, verify)
- Equity: banks typically expect at least the purchase costs (10–15%) to be paid from equity. The best rates need a loan-to-value of 60–80%. 100–110% financing for investors with good income is possible but costs roughly 0.3–0.8 percentage points more. Investment-property loans are sometimes priced slightly above owner-occupier loans.
- **vermögensverwaltende GmbH.** Corporate tax 15% plus Soli = 15.825%. The extended deduction (§ 9 Nr. 1 S. 2 GewStG) removes trade tax only if the company does nothing but manage its own real estate, and supplying furniture can endanger it. The 2025 Investitionssofortprogramm law cuts corporate tax by 1 point per year from 2028 to 10% in 2032. Downsides:
  - profit distributions are taxed again (flat 25% withholding tax);
  - no 10-year tax-free sale;
  - accounting costs of about €2–5k per year;
  - extra GrESt and notary costs when moving property in.

  It usually only makes sense for larger portfolios with profits retained in the company. For one to three student flats bought with debt and running tax losses in the early years, private ownership (losses offset against salary) is usually better.

### Gaps
- No official average rate series (e.g. Bundesbank MFI statistics for Sep/Oct 2026) was retrieved.
- Current KfW 261 terms were not verified on kfw.de.

## 7. Running costs: Hausgeld, Instandhaltungsrücklage, Grundsteuer, Mietausfallwagnis

### Takeaway
Owners bear the part of the Hausgeld that cannot be passed to tenants: the reserve contribution (Instandhaltungsrücklage) and the WEG administrator fee, plus maintenance on the flat itself and vacancy. Grundsteuer under the reform has applied since 2025. It is set by municipal Hebesatz and can be passed to tenants. Rule-of-thumb model inputs are given below; most are conventions rather than sourced statistics.

### Cited Findings
- The Instandhaltungsrücklage and administrator fee cannot be passed on via the operating-cost statement (Betriebskostenabrechnung) and stay with the owner. Include them in yield calculations ([gesetz-ratgeber.de](https://gesetz-ratgeber.de/rechtsgebiete/immobilienrecht/hausgeld-sonderumlage-weg), [ImmoScout24](https://www.immobilienscout24.de/wissen/vermieten/instandhaltungsruecklage.html)).
- Peters' formula: annual reserve per m² = construction cost per m² × 1.5 / 80. An alternative rule is 0.8–1.0% of construction cost per year ([Dr. Klein](https://www.drklein.de/finanzlexikon/instandhaltungsruecklage.html), [Homeday](https://www.homeday.de/de/immobilienwissen/instandhaltungsruecklage/)).
- The Grundsteuer reform took effect on 1 Jan 2025. Revenue neutrality was promised for municipalities overall, not for individual owners. Haus & Grund's survey of 1,999 owners found a 2025 median of €654 and a mean of about €830 per year. Berlin cut its Hebesatz from 810% to 470%. Sources: [Haus & Grund Sonderauswertung](https://www.hausundgrund.de/sites/default/files/downloads/grundsteuerreform-2025sonderauswertung.pdf), [Haus & Grund 23 Apr 2025](https://www.hausundgrund.de/sites/default/files/downloads/20250423-grundsteuerreform-2025-1.pdf), [mehrwertsteuerrechner Berlin](https://www.mehrwertsteuerrechner.de/grundsteuer/berlin/), [Volksstimme](https://www.volksstimme.de/sachsen-anhalt/grundsteuer-hebesatz-anstieg-eigentuemer-grundstueck-bescheid-3984532).

### Inferences (model conventions; general knowledge, verify)
- Grundsteuer can be passed to tenants as an operating cost (§ 2 Nr. 1 BetrKV). For WG and all-inclusive student rents it is effectively built into the rent. For a small flat, budget about €150–400 per year depending on the city.
- Typical model inputs as a share of cold rent:
  - non-recoverable Hausgeld: about 25–35% of total Hausgeld, or about €0.8–1.5/m² per month;
  - extra maintenance inside the flat: €5–10/m² per year (more for furnished: wear, replacement every 5–8 years);
  - Mietausfallwagnis: 2% of net cold rent, the ImmoWertV/BelWertV valuation convention. For student flats with turnover each semester, 3–5% is more realistic;
  - WEG/Sondereigentum administration: about €25–40 per month per unit if outsourced.
- Overall, non-recoverable costs of about 15–25% of gross rent are a common planning range for furnished student lets.
- Model formulas:
  - Net initial yield = (annual net cold rent − non-recoverable costs − vacancy allowance) / (price + purchase costs).
  - After-tax cash flow = rent − costs − interest − principal − tax. Tax = marginal rate × (rent − costs − interest − AfA on building − AfA on furniture).

### Gaps
- No authoritative source was found for the 2% Mietausfallwagnis or for typical non-recoverable shares of Hausgeld. These are industry conventions.
- No per-city Hebesatz data was collected for 2025/2026.
