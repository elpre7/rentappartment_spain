## 1. Mietpreisbremse and Kappungsgrenze: federal extension and coverage by Land and city

### Takeaway
**[IN FORCE]** The federal power for the Länder to impose the Mietpreisbremse runs until 31.12.2029. It took effect on 23.07.2025. As of early 2026, 13 of the 16 Länder have Mietpreisbremse ordinances. Saarland, Sachsen-Anhalt and Schleswig-Holstein have none, and Schleswig-Holstein only has a lowered 15% Kappungsgrenze. Most large university cities are covered. Three exceptions matter for the investor:
- Baden-Württemberg's ordinance expires on 31.12.2026.
- Hessen's ordinance expires on 25.11.2026. A new draft would cover 71 municipalities.
- Sachsen (Leipzig and Dresden) and Thüringen (Jena and Erfurt) were extended only until mid or end of 2027.

### Cited Findings
**Federal framework**
- The Bundestag extended the Mietpreisbremse on 26.06.2025 and the Bundesrat approved it on 11.07.2025. It has been in force since 23.07.2025 and now runs until 31.12.2029. Without it, the rule would have lapsed on 31.12.2025. — [mietspiegeltabelle.de](https://mietspiegeltabelle.de/mietpreisbremse-gilt-weiterhin-bis-ende-2029/); [Bundesregierung](https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/aktuelles/verlaengerung-mietpreisbremse-2350648); [NWB](https://datenbank.nwb.de/Dokument/1071855/); [BMJV PM 11.12.2024](https://www.bmjv.de/SharedDocs/Pressemitteilungen/DE/2024/1211_Mietpreisbremse.html)
- The previous 5-year time limit on Land ordinances was deleted. Search summary of [weltexpresso](https://weltexpresso.de/index.php/alltag/34705-verlaengerung-der-mietpreisbremse-bis-2029-weiter-ohne-substanzielle-nachbesserung) and [BBSR](https://www.bbsr.bund.de/BBSR/DE/forschung/fachbeitraege/wohnen-immobilien/mieten-preise/mietpreisbremse/01-start.html). **[UNCERTAIN]** I could not read the BBSR page directly.
- BBSR count for January 2026: 814 municipalities have a Mietpreisbremse ordinance, a Kappungsgrenze ordinance or both. 681 of them have both. **How the rules work**
- **Statute:** where an ordinance applies, the rent at the start of a new lease may not exceed the ortsübliche Vergleichsmiete (local comparative rent) plus 10% (§556d BGB).
- **Exceptions:**
  - A higher previous rent can be kept (§556e).
  - Flats first used and let after 1.10.2014 are exempt (§556f).
  - The first letting after comprehensive modernisation is exempt (§556f).
  - A landlord who relies on an exception must disclose it before the contract is signed (§556g Abs. 1a).
  - Sources: [§556d BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__556d.html); [§556f BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__556f.html)
- **Kappungsgrenze (§558 Abs. 3 BGB):** in an existing lease, rent increases up to the local comparative rent are capped at 20% in 3 years. A Land can lower this to 15% in designated areas. — [§558 BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__558.html)
- **Exemptions under §549 BGB:**
  - Student and youth halls of residence (Studenten- und Jugendwohnheime, §549 Abs. 3) are exempt from the Mietpreisbremse and from §575.
  - So is a furnished room inside a flat the landlord lives in, if let to a one-person household (§549 Abs. 2 Nr. 2).
  - Sources: [§549 BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__549.html); Studentenwerk model contract confirming §575 does not apply in Wohnheime ([Studentenwerk Würzburg](https://www.swerk-wue.de/fileadmin/imported_files/Daten/PDF_Wohnheime/Muster-Mietvertrag/wohnen_muster-mietvertrag_ba-wu_de.pdf))

### Inferences
- **Most restrictive markets:** Berlin, Hamburg, Munich and the rest of Bavaria, Bremen and the NRW university cities. They combine a Mietpreisbremse until about 2029, a 15% Kappungsgrenze, Zweckentfremdung rules (section 4) and, in Berlin and Munich, strict enforcement.
- **Least restrictive markets:** Halle and Magdeburg (Sachsen-Anhalt), Kiel (only the 15% cap) and Saarbrücken have no rent cap on new leases.
- **Medium or uncertain markets:**
  - Leipzig, Dresden, Jena and Erfurt are covered only until 2027. Renewal is likely given the political trend, but not certain.
  - BW and Hessen are each in a renewal gap. Renewal is very likely, since the federal power runs to 2029, but the list of municipalities may change.
- The Mietpreisbremse is the binding constraint for furnished room rents in WGs, because the furnished surcharge is currently a legal grey zone (section 2).

- I could not open the official BBSR list or the full Länder ordinance texts, so the Niedersachsen and RLP municipality lists are unresolved.
- BW renewal for 2027 or later is not found. The status of the Hessen cabinet decision after mid-November 2026 is unknown.
- Whether Regensburg, Erlangen, Bamberg, Gießen, Mainz and Trier are covered is not verified.

## 2. Furnished rentals, short-term and fixed-term contracts, Indexmiete and Schonfristzahlung: Mietrechtspaket II and the Expertenkommission

### Takeaway
**[PROPOSED, not yet law as of 18.10.2026]** The government draft "Mietrecht II" (BT-Drs. 21/6807) was approved by the cabinet on 29.04.2026. It had its first Bundestag reading on 9.07.2026 and a Rechtsausschuss hearing on 14.10.2026. No date has been set for the final vote. What it would do:
- Require the furnished surcharge to be shown separately and cap it. If it is not shown, the flat counts as unfurnished.
- Limit "vorübergehender Gebrauch" short-term leases to 6 months, or 8 in total with an extension.
- Damp Indexmiete increases above 3% inflation in tight markets.
- Extend Schonfristzahlung to ordinary termination.

The Expertenkommission is separately working on fines for Mietpreisbremse breaches and on Mietwucher (§5 WiStG). It has a deadline of 31.12.2026.

### Cited Findings
**Procedure**
- The cabinet adopted the draft on 29.04.2026. — [BMJV PM 29.04.2026](https://www.bmjv.de/SharedDocs/Pressemitteilungen/DE/2026/0429_Miete_II.html); [vhw](https://www.vhw.de/nachricht/mietrechtsreform-2026-bundesregierung-beschliesst-gesetzentwurf-fuer-erweiterten-mieterschutz/); [Tagesspiegel](https://www.tagesspiegel.de/politik/moblierte-wohnungen-und-kurzzeitvertrage-wie-die-bundesregierung-mieter-besser-schutzen-will-15540387.html)
- An earlier Referentenentwurf (February 2026) had a hard 3.5% Indexmiete cap. The cabinet draft replaced it with the "half above 3%" mechanism. — [infranken](https://www.infranken.de/ratgeber/karriere-geld/mietrecht-reform-2026-neue-regeln-fuer-indexmieten-und-moeblierte-wohnungen-art-6362156); [immowelt](https://www.immowelt.de/ratgeber/mieten/mietrechtsreform-2026)
- First reading was on 9.07.2026, with referral to the Rechtsausschuss. In that debate the CDU/CSU group already asked for changes in landlords' favour, especially on Schonfristzahlung and short-term leases. — [Bundestag Textarchiv kw28](https://www.bundestag.de/dokumente/textarchiv/2026/kw28-de-mieten-1192548); [bundestagszusammenfasser](https://bundestagszusammenfasser.de/details?docid=1148); [hib](https://www.bundestag.de/presse/hib/kurzmeldungen-1193282)

Experts were divided: tenant groups wanted more, owner associations warned against over-regulation. — [Bundestag kw42](https://www.bundestag.de/dokumente/textarchiv/2026/kw42-pa-recht-mieten-1221718); [rechtundpolitik](https://www.rechtundpolitik.com/politik/rechtspolitik/mietrechtsreform-sachverstaendige-im-bundestag-uneins-ueber-geplante-neuregelungen/); [Committee agenda](https://www.bundestag.de/resource/blob/1222846/to_057-pdf.pdf)
- A timetable of 26.09.2026 shows the committee recommendation, the 2nd and 3rd readings and the Bundesrat stage as "kein Termin bekannt" (no date known). Earlier expectations of entry into force in autumn 2026 are unconfirmed. Until then current law applies. — search summary citing [RAV](https://www.rav.de/publikationen/mitteilungen/mitteilung/gesetzesentwurf-der-bundesregierung-bt-drs-21-6807-ua-mietrechtspaket-ii-1305) and [Mietervereinigung Berlin](https://www.mietervereinigung-berlin.de/news/mietrechtspaket-ii-moeblierte-wohnungen-mietpreisbremse.html)

**Furnished surcharge (as drafted)**
- Under the Mietpreisbremse, the local comparative rent could be raised by an "angemessener Möblierungszuschlag" (reasonable furnishing surcharge).
- The cap would be at most 1% per month of the estimated current value (Zeitwert) of the furniture.
- Alternatively, for fully furnished flats, a flat rate of 10% of the net cold rent is presumed reasonable.
- The surcharge must be shown separately. If it is not, the flat counts as let unfurnished. The landlord would also have to disclose information on request.
- It would apply to new contracts in Mietpreisbremse areas.
- Sources: [bundestagszusammenfasser](https://bundestagszusammenfasser.de/details?docid=1148); [infranken](https://www.infranken.de/ratgeber/karriere-geld/mietrecht-reform-2026-neue-regeln-fuer-indexmieten-und-moeblierte-wohnungen-art-6362156); [lpe-immobilien](https://lpe-immobilien.com/immobilien-vermieten/moebliertes-wohnen/)

**Short-term leases (as drafted)**
- Leases for "vorübergehender Gebrauch" under §549 Abs. 2 Nr. 1 BGB, which are exempt from the social protections of tenancy law, would be capped at 6 months. They could be extended once to 8 months in total.
- They would be allowed only where the tenant has a special need for temporary use.
- Sources: [Tagesspiegel](https://www.tagesspiegel.de/politik/moblierte-wohnungen-und-kurzzeitvertrage-wie-die-bundesregierung-mieter-besser-schutzen-will-15540387.html); [vermieter1](https://vermieter1.de/blog/mietrechtsreform-2026); [regiomanager](https://www.regiomanager.de/artikel/zeitwohnen-unter-beschuss/)

**Indexmiete (as drafted)**
- If consumer prices rise by more than 3% in a year, only half of the excess could be passed on.
- This would apply only in tight markets designated by a Land ordinance. One source says existing contracts would also be covered once such an ordinance is in force.
- Sources: [immowelt](https://www.immowelt.de/ratgeber/mieten/mietrechtsreform-2026); [anwalt.de](https://www.anwalt.de/rechtstipps/mietrecht-ii-2026-was-der-neue-gesetzentwurf-fuer-mieter-und-vermieter-bedeutet-266141.html)

**Schonfristzahlung (as drafted)**
- At present, paying the arrears within the grace period cures only an extraordinary termination for rent arrears (§569 Abs. 3 Nr. 2 BGB).
- The draft would extend this to ordinary termination for arrears.
- Sources: [buerger-geld.org](https://www.buerger-geld.org/news/finanzen/mietschulden-mieter-sollen-kuenftig-auch-ordentliche-kuendigung-abwenden-koennen); [§569 BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__569.html)

**Transition (as drafted)**
- Contracts concluded before the law takes effect keep the old rules on short-term leases, the furnished surcharge and disclosure.
- The short-term cap would apply from the first day of the fourth month after promulgation.
- Sources: [bundestagszusammenfasser](https://bundestagszusammenfasser.de/details?docid=1148); [vermieter1](https://vermieter1.de/blog/mietrechtsreform-2026)

**Expertenkommission Mietrecht**
- It started on 16.09.2025.
- Its focus is fines for Mietpreisbremse breaches, a reform of Mietwucher under §5 WiStG, and harmonising tenancy rules. Proposals are due by 31.12.2026.
- Its deliberations are confidential. The government currently has no plan to reform criminal Wucher under §291 StGB.
- Sources: [BMJV PM 16.09.2025](https://www.bmjv.de/SharedDocs/Pressemitteilungen/DE/2025/0916_Mietrecht_Expertenkommission_Auftakt.html); [Bundestag hib](https://www.bundestag.de/presse/hib/kurzmeldungen-1210186); [BT-Drs. 21/7718](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/077/2107718.pdf); [BT-Drs. 21/7523](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/075/2107523.pdf)

**Current law [IN FORCE]**
- Furnishing alone does not remove the flat from the social protections of tenancy law. What counts is a specific purpose for temporary use that both parties recognise, such as a work assignment or a trade fair.
- The deposit cap of 3 net cold rents (§551 BGB) applies in all cases.
- Sources: [BMJV brochure "Möblierter Wohnraum und vorübergehender Gebrauch"](https://www.bmjv.de/SharedDocs/Publikationen/DE/Broschueren/Moeblierter_Wohnraum.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1); [Mieterbund / Hinz](https://mieterbund.de/app/uploads/2025/02/8-Werner-Hinz-Wohnraummietverhaeltnisse-nach-%C2%A7-549-Abs.-2-BGB.pdf); [malkus.lawyer](https://malkus.lawyer/2024/10/wann-gilt-wohnraum-als-nur-zu-einem-voruebergehenden-gebrauch-vermietet-%C2%A7-549-abs-2-nr-1-bgb/)

### Inferences
- **Business-model risk.** The "furnished room at a premium" model would be hit in Mietpreisbremse cities. The room rent would have to be split into the base rent (capped at Vergleichsmiete + 10%) and a furnishing surcharge that can be checked. The flat 10% presumption is a useful ceiling for modelling: total furnished rent of about Vergleichsmiete × 1.1 × 1.1.
- **Semester contracts no longer work under §549 Abs. 2 Nr. 1.** A student who studies for years is generally not in "vorübergehender Gebrauch" already today. The 6-month cap would make this explicit. Model WG rooms as regular, protected leases.
- **The bill is likely to pass, but details may soften.** Expect the Bundestag to pass it in late 2026 or 2027, possibly with changes such as more room for short-term leases and Schonfrist. This is an inference based on the CDU/CSU's demands at first reading.
- **Fines may follow.** Fines for overcharging under the Mietpreisbremse could follow from the Expertenkommission in 2027 or later, which raises enforcement risk.

### Gaps
- I have no official draft text, so the exact wording on WG rooms, Wohnheime and partially furnished flats is unknown. I found nothing on whether §549 Abs. 3 (Wohnheime) changes.
- The vote date and any committee amendments after 14.10.2026 are not yet known.

## 3. Legal structures for student WGs and fixed-term contracts

### Takeaway
**[IN FORCE]** There are three common structures:
1. Hauptmieter with Untermieter (one main tenant who sublets the other rooms)
2. Einzelmietverträge, one contract per room
3. One joint contract with all flatmates as Gesamtschuldner (jointly and severally liable)

A private WG flat almost never qualifies as a "Studentenwohnheim" (§549 Abs. 3) or as "vorübergehender Gebrauch". Full tenant protection therefore applies:
- the Mietpreisbremse
- §573 grounds required for the landlord to terminate
- §575 Zeitmietvertrag only with a statutory reason, which "tenant is a student" is not

### Cited Findings
**Hauptmieter / Untermieter**
- The main tenant is liable for the whole rent even if a room is empty. The landlord can only claim against the main tenant.
- Advantage: one point of contact. Risk: no joint liability of the other flatmates.
- Ending the arrangement is cumbersome, because the subtenancies have to be terminated first and then the main lease.
- Sources: [immoportal](https://www.immoportal.com/vermieten/mietvertrag/wg-mietvertrag-was-mieter-und-vermieter-wissen-sollten); [Haus & Grund WG-Mietvertrag](https://www.hausundgrund.de/wg-mietvertrag-worauf-vermieter-achten-muessen)
- A furnished room sublet inside a flat the main tenant lives in can be terminated by the 15th of a month for the end of that month (§549 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 together with §573c Abs. 3). — [anwaltonline](https://www.anwaltonline.com/mietrecht/tipps/1301/untervermietung-unmoebliertes-und-moebliertes-zimmer); [§573c BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__573c.html)

**Einzelmietverträge**
- More administration for the landlord. Each tenant is liable only for their own room. This structure is mainly common in halls of residence.
- Sources: [immoportal](https://www.immoportal.com/vermieten/mietvertrag/wg-mietvertrag-was-mieter-und-vermieter-wissen-sollten); [immowelt WG-Mietvertrag](https://www.immowelt.de/ratgeber/vermieten/wg-mietvertrag); [ImmoScout24](https://www.immobilienscout24.de/wissen/vermieten/wg-mietvertrag.html)

**Joint contract (Gesamtschuldner)**
- All flatmates sign and are jointly and severally liable. Changing a flatmate needs the landlord's agreement.
- Source: [Haus & Grund](https://www.hausundgrund.de/wg-mietvertrag)

**Statute texts**
- **§575 BGB:** a fixed-term lease is valid only for listed reasons: own use, substantial modernisation or demolition, or a tenancy tied to employment. Otherwise the lease counts as indefinite. — [§575 BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__575.html)
- **§549 Abs. 3 BGB:** in a student or youth hall of residence, §§556d–561, 573, 573a, 573d, 575, 575a and others do not apply. — [§549 BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__549.html)
- In a Wohnheim model contract, §575 is expressly excluded under §549 Abs. 3. — [Studentenwerk Würzburg](https://www.swerk-wue.de/fileadmin/imported_files/Daten/PDF_Wohnheime/Muster-Mietvertrag/wohnen_muster-mietvertrag_ba-wu_de.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Wohnheim exemption.** Case law requires a real Wohnheim: a building suited and intended for students, usually with a rotation principle. A single WG flat does not qualify. Calling a private flat a "Studentenwohnheim" is a high-risk practice.
- **Usual landlord practice for 3-4 room student flats:**
  - Einzelmietverträge for unfurnished or furnished rooms on indefinite leases. These allow rent per room and replacing tenants one at a time, with no joint liability.
  - Or a joint contract with all flatmates, plus a clause allowing tenant replacement.
  - A mutual waiver of ordinary termination for up to 4 years (Kündigungsverzicht) is a common substitute for a fixed term.
  - Under the Mietpreisbremse, the rent per room must be benchmarked against the Mietspiegel for the whole flat, apportioned to each room.
- **Hauptmieter model.** It shifts letting risk to one student but leaves the landlord exposed to a single debtor. The main tenant also needs the landlord's permission to sublet (§540, §553 BGB).

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve a source on the BGH case law on Kündigungsverzicht or on a Mietpreisbremse calculation per WG room. The answers above are standard doctrine, not checked against a source in this session.

## 4. Zweckentfremdungsverbote (bans on using housing for other purposes) and their effect on furnished and short-term lets

### Takeaway
**[IN FORCE]** Berlin, Munich, Hamburg, Frankfurt, Köln, Bonn, Münster, Aachen, Düsseldorf, Dortmund, Stuttgart and Freiburg have bans on using housing for other purposes:
- Holiday or short-stay letting needs a permit or registration ID.
- Exceptions apply for the owner's own main residence, typically up to 8 weeks or 90 days a year.
- Fines reach €500,000, or €100,000 in BW.

A WG let on ordinary residential leases is housing use, so it is not affected. Furnished lets on a daily or weekly basis in these cities are high-risk. Since 20.05.2026 the EU short-term rental regulation (2024/1028) has added registration and data-sharing duties.

### Cited Findings
- **Overview table from a 2026 guide:**
  - Berlin: €500k, 90 days a year
  - München: €500k, 8 weeks a year
  - Hamburg: €500k
  - Köln: €500k, Satzung since 2021
  - Frankfurt: €500k, Hessian housing supervision law
  - Stuttgart and Freiburg: €100k, BW ZwEWG
  - Further cities with Satzungen: Bonn, Münster, Dortmund
  - Source: [nutzungsänderung.de](https://xn--nutzungsnderung-7kb.de/zweckentfremdung-wohnraum); [kanzleimauss](https://www.kanzleimauss.de/zweckentfremdung/)
- **Berlin:** the Zweckentfremdungsverbot law was amended in 2026 to fit the EU short-term rental regulation. Permit fee: €100 for a main residence and €150 for a second residence, valid 2 years. A second residence is limited to 90 days a year. — [Berlin Senat PM 2026](https://www.berlin.de/rbmskzl/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2026/pressemitteilung.1658086.php); [hnts.legal](https://hnts.legal/post/zweckentfremdungsverbot-und-eu-meldepflicht---was-sich-in-berlin-ab-mai-2026-andert)
- **München:**
  - Holiday letting without a permit is limited to 8 weeks a year, and registration is required before advertising.
  - **[UNCERTAIN]** One source says a new Satzung applies from 1.08.2026, another says registration has been required since 2018.
  - Source: [nebenkostenrechneronline](https://www.nebenkostenrechneronline.de/blog/kurzzeitvermietung-airbnb-regeln)
- **Hamburg:** permit required under the Wohnraumschutzgesetz. Exception for a main residence up to 8 weeks a year. — [nebenkostenrechneronline](https://www.nebenkostenrechneronline.de/blog/kurzzeitvermietung-airbnb-regeln)
- **NRW:** a housing ID (Wohnraum-ID) has been required since 1.1.2023 in cities with a Satzung: Aachen, Bonn, Dortmund, Düsseldorf, Köln and Münster. — [nebenkostenrechneronline](https://www.nebenkostenrechneronline.de/blog/kurzzeitvermietung-airbnb-regeln)

### Inferences
- **Student WG on 6–12 month or longer leases:** no Zweckentfremdung issue.
- **Re-letting rooms over the summer or between semesters on Airbnb:** a problem in Berlin, Munich, Hamburg and the NRW and BW cities above. Leaving a flat empty for long periods is also a Zweckentfremdung in some cities, typically after 3–6 months.
- **Most restrictive enforcement:** Berlin and Munich.

## 5. Other cost and regulatory factors: Grundsteuer, energy-efficiency law (GEG, now GModG) and Mietspiegel

### Takeaway
**[IN FORCE]**
- **Grundsteuer:** the new property tax has applied since 1.1.2025. It is collected under either the federal model or a Land model and can be passed on to tenants as an operating cost. It shifts the burden between properties, and in some city locations it rose sharply.
- **Heating law:** the 2024 "Heizungsgesetz" (GEG) has been replaced by the Gebäudemodernisierungsgesetz (GModG), with the main rules in force from 29.07.2026. The 65%-renewables duty on heating replacement is abolished, which lowers capex risk for older flats. A green-gas quota is still pending.
- **Mietspiegel:** municipalities with more than 50,000 inhabitants must publish a Mietspiegel (§558c Abs. 4 BGB). This makes it easier to check Mietpreisbremse compliance.

### Cited Findings
**Grundsteuer**
- Land models are used in Bayern, BW, Hamburg, Hessen, Niedersachsen and Saarland. The rest use the federal model. Berlin uses the federal model; one source wavered on this. — [ftimmobilien24](https://www.ftimmobilien24.com/immobilie-verkaufen/grundsteuer-reform-kaufpreise-muenchen-deutschland/); [AUTOHAUS SteuerLuchs](https://www.autohaus.de/recht-steuern/steuerluchs/autohaus-steuerluchs-grundsteuerreform-der-aktuelle-stand-2890839)
- Bayern uses an area-based model: €0.04/m² of land and €0.50/m² of building, with a reduced rate for residential use. — [ftimmobilien24](https://www.ftimmobilien24.com/immobilie-verkaufen/grundsteuer-reform-kaufpreise-muenchen-deutschland/)
- The reform was designed to be revenue-neutral in total. In some Berlin locations the tax rose by 50–200%. It is passed on through the operating-cost statement. — [Berliner Zeitung](https://www.berliner-zeitung.de/article/223625); [Bund der Steuerzahler](https://steuerzahler.de/presse/detail/vergleich-der-wohnnebenkosten-in-den-landeshauptstaedten/)
- **[UNCERTAIN]** The BFH held the federal model constitutional in December 2025. Challenges to Land models are still pending. — search summary; primary source not retrieved.

**GModG (replacing the GEG)**
- Cabinet approval 13.05.2026, Bundestag vote 10.07.2026 (323 to 271), Bundesrat approval without the mediation committee.
- Promulgated in July 2026. Core rules in force from 29.07.2026, with further stages on 1.1.2027, 1.1.2028 and 1.1.2030.
- The 65%-renewables duty and the 30-year boiler replacement duty were dropped.
- A green-gas quota from 2028 is to be set by a separate law, targeted for 1.12.2026.
- Sources: [Haufe](https://www.haufe.de/immobilien/wirtschaft-politik/gebaeudemodernisierungsgesetz-gmg_84342_683694.html); [GModG-Infoportal (Bund)](https://www.gmodg.bund.de/GModG/DE/Home/startseite/GModG_News/GModG_News-node.html); [Bundesregierung](https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/aktuelles/neues-gebaeudemodernisierungsgesetz-2430284); [Görg 17.07.2026](https://www.goerg.de/de/aktuelles/veroeffentlichungen/17-07-2026/gebaeudemodernisierungsgesetz-gmodg-schafft-geg-ab-was-sich-aendert-und-was-bleibt); [Ebner Stolz](https://www.ebnerstolz.de/de/unser-angebot/leistungen/rechtsberatung/energierecht/gebaeudemodernisierungsgesetz-verabschiedet-109427.html)

**Mietspiegel**
- Duty for municipalities with more than 50,000 inhabitants under §558c Abs. 4 BGB, from the Mietspiegelreformgesetz.
- Deadlines were 1.1.2023 for a simple Mietspiegel and 1.1.2024 for a qualified one.
- In Bavaria 17 cities are affected.
- Sources: [IWW](https://www.iww.de/fmp/aktuelle-gesetzgebung/mietrecht-neue-regeln-fuer-mietspiegel-f142735); [Haufe](https://www.haufe.de/immobilien/wirtschaft-politik/mietspiegel-reform-immobilienbranche-gegen-ausweitung_84342_484664.html); [Bayer. Justizministerium draft](https://www.justiz.bayern.de/media/pdf/gesetze/verordnungsentwurf_zur_Änderung_der_zuständigkeitsverordnung_samt_vorblatt_und_begründung.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Energy-efficiency risk for older flats:** lower than under the GEG 2024. Remaining cost drivers:
  - the CO2 price on heating, with the cost split between landlord and tenant by building efficiency class under the CO2KostAufG (not researched here)
  - the EU Buildings Directive (EPBD), which must be transposed by 2026 and may later bring renovation pressure
  - the green-gas quota
- **Grundsteuer:** a pass-through cost. It mainly affects WG affordability and gross rent, not the landlord's net yield, as long as it is properly allocated in the lease.
